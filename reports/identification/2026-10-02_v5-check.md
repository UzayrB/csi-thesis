## 4. For the decision (my reading)

- **For the change:** simpler, works straight away, independent of earlier walks; natural +2.78 and towards +4.44; the
  substitution test would follow the radio fully, by construction.
- **Against it:** the natural gain is not separable from zero; Room B tracking misses its margin by one walk (-4.58
  [-19.58, +11.67]) and Room A falls by 1.67; slow sits exactly at its floor; the multi-walk measures fall sharply (5
  walks -16.67, round trip -6.67); and the lean towards PA1 grows (59 named; 17 of the top 30 %'s 29 right answers).
- **Recommendation:** keep engine v4. The candidate's case rests on a natural gain inside the noise, while the measures
  that combine walks, and the tracking walks, get worse. If simplicity is worth more than those, adopting it would be a
  decision against the brief's own check, and the thesis would have to say so.
