## 6. Part 5: engine v4, built and verified

**What v4 is** (PLAN note 2 Oct 00:23): the window is [t0, t0 + 4.0 s]; the direction is read on its first 3 s, as v3
does; the end detector (RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy at causal checkpoints) ends the walking span; the Wi-Fi speed is
measured on that span; features and maps use the whole window; a window stops at a pending press. The end detector's
alpha is a model parameter in each manifest (0.2 for `loso_B1_v4`, fitted on B2; 0.5 otherwise). The CNN files are
v3's, copied.

**Checks:**
- the v4 bank, built through the engine's own Extractor at both alphas, equals the screen's 4.0 s bank (features, maps,
  decision times, speeds, direction) on all 1,019 windows;
- the five fold replays equal the screen's prediction: B2 299 of 299, B1 360 of 360, A1, A2 and A3 120 of 120 each;
  largest ensemble difference 4.3e-14 (`runs/identify/engine/v4_prediction_check.json`);
- the rig path (recorded B2 through `RecordedSource`, `EngineConsumer`, `EngineApp`): 299 of 299 decisions, press times
  equal, ensemble difference 0.0 (`runs/identify/teaching/verify_B2_v4.json`);
- tests: `tests/test_ide_rigrun.py` and `tests/test_ide_engine.py`, 13 passed with `IDE_SLOW=1` (582 s).

## 7. What went wrong

- **The laptop ran out of memory at about 22:10 on 1 Oct.** Five MPS component jobs ran in parallel and grew without
  bound. Since then: one GPU job at a time under a watchdog (`runs/identify/lean_in/queue/guard.sh`: 30 % free-memory
  floor, a per-job footprint cap), MPS watermark caps, and `torch.mps.empty_cache()` per step.
- **The watchdog's 6 GB cap stopped the B2 v4 replay** (its footprint reached 6.2 GB with 75 % free); the cap for
  replays is 9 GB.
- **A background command was killed after 30 minutes** (the tool's limit), so long queues run detached with `nohup`.
- **Times typed before reading the clock** in two log lines were corrected.
