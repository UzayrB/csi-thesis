## 1. The GS confirmation (declared test, run once)

**The rule** (PLAN section 1, committed before any training). GS is adopted only if:
1. the pooled paired gain over the 600 tracking passes has a 95 % interval over participants entirely
   above zero;
2. neither room loses more than 3 points.

**The protocol:**
- leave one session out, CAL-causal, CNNs at 6,000 steps with 3 seeds;
- run once, 09:19 to 10:19;
- the baseline is B1's saved `final_loso_*` outputs, not retrained;
- the B1 and B2 folds reuse the audit's GS models, whose training rows are identical (asserted).

| | pooled (600) | Room A (360) | Room B (240) |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1: the bug-fixed final configuration | 35.67 [25.00, 44.17] | 39.44 | 30.00 |
| B1+GS | 33.67 [23.00, 42.50] | 36.67 | 29.17 |
| **paired change** | **-2.00 [-4.00, +0.50]** | -2.78 [-8.89, +1.94] | -0.83 [-9.17, +7.50] |

- **Decision:** condition 1 fails and condition 2 holds, so **GS is not adopted**. The engine uses B1's
  signal path.
- **For information:**
  - the participant-session cluster bootstrap gives -2.00 [-9.83, +6.00];
  - per session: A1 -0.83, A2 -7.50, A3 0.00, B1 +0.83, B2 -2.50;
  - per participant, from P1 to P6: -4, -4, 0, -2, +3, -5.
- **For the thesis:** GS's +10.00 [-5.00, 23.33] on the identification walks (audit item 17) was seen in
  the audit. It does not carry over to the tracking passes, where GS is slightly worse.

## 2. The engine, step by step (fixed in the PLAN before any training)

`pipeline/identify/engine.py`. The bank builder (`ide_bank`), the evaluation harness (`ide_replay`) and the
live app (`pipeline/live/identify_engine.py`) all call it. The training bank is built by the engine's own
extraction, and every replayed window's features and maps were checked equal to the bank's: 2,337 of
2,337 across the nine replays.

| step | what happens | fixed parameters |
|---|---|---|
| 1. stream | each receiver's frames are pushed in arrival order into a rolling buffer | 60 s buffer |
| 2. clock | per receiver, causal offset = 5th percentile of (t_mono minus local time) over the frames that arrived in the trailing 20 s; a wrap is unwrapped; any other backwards jump is a reboot and restarts the estimate | 20 s; at least 20 frames, otherwise t_mono |
| 3. press | the space bar sets t0; the window is [t0, t0 + T] | **T = 3.0 s** |
| 4. wait | the decision time is the first frame arrival at which every receiver has a frame past t0 + T on the causal clock, or t0 + T + 1 s | cap 1.0 s |
| 5. extract | the window on a 150 Hz grid; the 630 v2 gait features (`id19_featbank.pass_features`) and the Doppler maps (`idz_bank.conditioned_map_v2`) | the bank's own functions |
| 6. direction | the sign of the least-squares slope of log RX2 10 to 35 Hz energy over the window (falling means away) | used, since at least 95 % accurate (section 4) |
| 7. speed | v = f_torso x 0.123 / (2 F), on the link with the largest mean bistatic factor F for the path and layout; NaN if F < 0.3 | the single pace column of the three pace components |
| 8. calibrate | every earlier window of the session, unlabelled: feature standardisation (at least 10), AdaBN (at least 1); updated after each decision, so never on the critical path | causal |
| 9. decide | the eight components' log-probabilities summed; the argmax; displayed confidence softmax(sum / 8) | equal weights |

**The model:**
- the eight components are unchanged from the audit's final configuration: 3 random forests, extra
  trees, shrinkage LDA, CNN-v2 plain and AdaBN, per-link CNNs;
- they are retrained on the engine's bank, which holds every pass's first 3 s (tracking passes
  included);
- the pace column is the Wi-Fi speed.

**Tracking passes** are replayed with the route known (proposed at 09:05, accepted with Uzayr's go-ahead): the line and direction
come from the recording, while the window, the speed and the calibration are the engine's.

## 9. The live app

**On the rig** (three receivers over serial; RX2 at the identification spot beside the TX):

```
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.live rig --mode identify [--minutes 10] [--device mps]
```

- **Model:** it loads the deployment model `runs/identify/engine/models/deploy_all5` (every pass of all
  five sessions) from its `manifest.json`, and refuses without it.
- **Keys:**
  - SPACE when the walker starts on line A;
  - `n` marks a new walker (the "k walks" tally);
  - `q` stops.
- **Warm-up:** a few walks by several people first. The screen shows "calibrating: n walks so far"
  until 10.

**A render** (a recorded session through the app at real speed, for screen recording):

```
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.live replay-identify --session B2 --realtime --tally
```

- **Model:** it loads the fold model that never saw the replayed day (`loso_B2` for B2, `loso_B1` for
  B1, `loso_A*` for Room A) and names it on screen, with "REPLAY".
- **Presses:** each recorded walk's start press is replayed as the one press; every other press is
  ignored.
- **Snapshots:** `--snapshots <dir>` saves the screen after each decision instead of playing in a
  window. `runs/live/identify/snapshots_B2_check/` holds ten, from B2's first recording.
- **The dispatcher hook:** tracking added identification's three-line hook to `pipeline/live/__main__.py`,
  at identification's request, and committed it verbatim in 63987d1. The same command also runs as
  `python3 -m pipeline.live.identify_engine replay-identify ...`.

**The screen per walk:**
- the detected direction (arrow);
- the top identity and its displayed confidence, and the runner-up, against the chance line;
- Wi-Fi speed and latency;
- the model;
- "closed set: six enrolled people";
- "calibrating" under 10 walks;
- "REPLAY";
- a round-trip panel when a towards walk follows an away walk within 15 s.

**Output:** one JSON line per decision under `runs/live/identify/` (`reports/ID_LIVE_INTERFACE.md`).

**Tests** (`tests/test_ide_engine.py`, 6 of 6 passed):
- the causal clock tells a wrap from a reboot;
- one press gives one decision;
- batching never changes a decision;
- **the app's replay equals the harness's decisions pass for pass**;
- the deployment model loads from its manifest;
- the legacy mode's gate 5 (below).

**The legacy random-forest mode is unchanged:** gate 5 passes (live extraction and prediction equal the frozen `id29` batch on its synthetic recordings; `IDE_SLOW=1 ... -k legacy`, 249 s, rerun at 13:57 after the quiet window).

## 10. Cadence

On the 55 natural identification walks whose Doppler-band energy envelope shows a clear step or stride
rhythm:
- the feature bank's resolved cadence agrees with the envelope's within 10 % on 29 % of walks;
- it is half or double it (step against stride) on 0 %;
- the ratio's median is 0.88 (interquartile 0.76 to 0.95).

**So the resolver does not confuse step with stride; it runs low, by a median 12 %.**

   3 s window, a decision a median 0.42 s after the window (usually before the walk ends), direction and
   speed from the signal.

## 12. Identification is frozen

**The engine is the model.** The engine is `pipeline/identify/engine.py`, with the eight-component
ensemble retrained on its own bank and B1's signal path (no GS).

**Every thesis figure** for identification comes from replaying the recorded sessions through this engine
(`ide_replay`, then `ide_eval`), with the same code, causal timing and models as the live app. The app's
replay was tested equal to the harness pass for pass.

**The saved models the app uses:**
- the rig: `runs/identify/engine/models/deploy_all5`, from its manifest (trained on all five sessions,
  never scored);
- replays and renders: `loso_B2` for B2, `loso_B1` for B1 and `loso_A1` to `loso_A3` for Room A, each
  never trained on the day it replays.

**No further identification development happens.** The earlier records (`ID_AUDIT`, `ID_WALKS`,
`ID_DEV_FINAL`) stay as the two-press reference.

**For Uzayr:**
1. The one-press design costs 8.6 points on Room A's tracking walks (clear), and 3.9 on the
   identification walks (not separable).
2. The engine's advantage over speed on fast walks is no longer separable from zero. The thesis framing
   of gait against pace needs your call.
3. The render is ready to screen-record (section 9).

## 13. What went wrong

- **A time written before the clock was read.** A PLAN note was headed 09:52; the clock read 09:49. A
  correction note was appended (05863b2).
- **The check-ins did not fire.** The 30-minute check-ins were scheduled at 10:50 (session scheduler),
  but did not fire while the lanes ran (10:20 to 13:39). The check-in log records the gap. Nothing
  failed in it: the lanes ran and finished cleanly, and tracking held no training.
- **Deviations from the PLAN, each recorded as a dated note before it happened:**
  - the six banks were built before the GS decision;
  - the jobs ran in two lanes instead of one at a time (agreed with tracking).
- **The extractor was changed after the bank build.** Idle polls now drop old blocks instead of copying
  the buffer. The change touches no decision, and every replayed window still equals the bank (2,337 of
  2,337).
- **The first automatic cadence sentence misdescribed the ratio** (as scatter). It was corrected before
  commit.
- **The first synthetic reboot in the clock test** jumped back by less than 1 s. It was corrected before
  the test first ran.
- **The legacy gate-5 test was stopped by PID at 13:42** to give tracking a quiet machine. It was then
  rerun (section 9).

## 14. Limits
