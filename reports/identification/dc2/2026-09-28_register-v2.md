## A. The frozen SECONDARY's defects (its committed rows stand as scored; none is rescored)

- The tensor has 56 time columns but a 3.0 s crop fills 53 (hop 8 samples at 150 Hz), so columns 53
  to 55 are always zero.
- Passes shorter than 3.0 s are zero-padded, so the padding length encodes pace.
- 60 training passes at batch 48 give 120 optimiser steps, and the second batch of each epoch holds 12
  samples.
- The augmentation is drawn once per batch, not per sample: every sample of a batch gets the same
  shift and the same time and frequency masks.

## 4. The configurations (fixed; every one is run once and reported)

**Data used for training.** For cross-day, the training day's passes in **both directions and at all three paces:**
- day 1: 20 `A_fwd` plus 20 `A_rev` per participant, 240 passes in all;
- day 2: 30 per participant, 180 passes, minus the one excluded pass.

Direction is an input, and reverse passes are never time-reversed.

### N1, CNN-v2 (non-transductive)
- **Conditioning.** Identical to the frozen chain up to the STFT: amplitude; subcarrier selection to 103; the clock map; linear resampling to 150 Hz between the presses; the 4 to 70 Hz fourth-order Butterworth, zero-phase, over the whole pass; per-pass PCA, keeping PCs 2 to 4 with the skew sign rule; STFT with nperseg 32, noverlap 24 and nfft 64; the 28 bins from 4.69 to 67.97 Hz; summed magnitude over the PCs; log(mag + 1e-6); minus the mean of that link's whole-pass map.
- **Windows.** 34 STFT frames (about 2.0 s), hop 4 frames, taken **from inside the pass with no padding ever**. A pass with fewer than 34 frames is excluded and counted in the report.
- **Normalisation.** Per-link z-score with training-side mean and SD, computed separately for each direction.
- **Architecture.**
  - Three blocks, each Conv2d 3x3 with padding 1, then BatchNorm, then ReLU: 3 to 16, 16 to 32 and 32 to 32 channels.
  - MaxPool **over time only**, (1, 2), after blocks 1 and 2.
  - Then the mean over time, which keeps the frequency axis, giving 32 x 28.
  - Flatten, concatenate a two-value direction one-hot, dropout 0.3, then Linear to 6.
  - Report the parameter count.
- **Training.**
  - Adam, learning rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, batch 64 windows, **3,000 steps**, cosine decay to 0, class-balanced sampling.
  - **Per-sample** augmentation:
    - one time mask of up to 4 frames;
    - one frequency mask of up to 2 bins;
    - a per-link log-gain offset drawn from N(0, 0.2);
    - each link dropped with probability 0.15;
    - noise of 0.02 N(0, 1).
  - **Forbidden:** circular shift, time stretch, resampling and frequency shift.
  - Seeds 0 to 4.
- **Prediction.**
  - The mean log-softmax over all windows of a pass, then the mean over seeds.
  - Also report each seed's accuracy, with the mean and SD.

### N1-A, CNN-v2 with AdaBN (transductive, declared)
N1's trained weights, with the BatchNorm statistics recomputed on the **test day's other blocks** (every test-day block except the one being scored), with no labels and no gradients. The deployment reading is "calibrate on today's other walks".

### N2, RF-v2 (non-transductive)
The frozen PRIMARY features (`id19_featbank.pass_features`, U_all_gait) and head (random forest, 300 trees, `max_features` sqrt, `random_state` 0), trained on all training-day passes, with training-side median imputation and z-scoring per path (`A_fwd` and `A_rev` separately).

### N2-D, RF-v2 with per-day standardisation (transductive, declared)
The test day's features are standardised with statistics from that day's **other blocks**, pooled across all participants.

### N3, S_csi: pace sensed by Wi-Fi (control)
- **Measurement.** For each pass, take the TX-RX2 torso contour (the 50th-percentile energy frequency per frame from the conditioned spectrogram) and its median f_t. Estimate speed as v_hat = f_t x lambda / (2 F), with F the path-mean projected factor for TX-RX2 from `id15_factor_map.py` and lambda from the channel.
- **Classifier.** Shrinkage LDA on v_hat alone.
- **Report** the correlation of v_hat with press speed on the training day.

### N4, INTEGRATED
The equal-weight sum of the log-probabilities of N1-A, N2-D and N3. The weights are fixed at 1, 1 and 1 and are never tuned.

### N4g, INTEGRATED without pace
The equal-weight sum of N1-A and N2-D. This is the gait-only system.

### Ablations of N1 (each reported, none selected)
- **N1-f:** speed-normalised frequency axis. Each pass's frequency bins are rescaled by that pass's f_t to a common reference before windowing. It shows how much of N1 is pace.
- **N1-rx2:** the TX-RX2 channel only.
- **N1-fwd:** trained on `A_fwd` passes only, which shows the effect of the added data.

### Controls on every split
- **S0:** press pace, shrinkage LDA, as frozen.
- **RECSIG:** static levels (mean amplitude per subcarrier and mean RSSI over the pass), shrinkage LDA, as frozen.
- **A permutation null** of 1,000 label permutations over participants.

## 5. Evaluations

### E1, across days
Train on day 1 and test on day 2, and the reverse. Score each on two test sets:
- **(i)** natural `A_fwd` only (n = 30 for day 1 to day 2, and 60 for the reverse), comparable with confirmatory analysis P1;
- **(ii)** every test-day pass (about 179 and 240), with rows per block (natural, slow, fast) and per direction.

### E2, multiple walks
- One decision per participant per block, summing calibrated log-probabilities over the block's passes. The temperature is fitted on the training day's out-of-fold probabilities.
- One decision per cycle (an `A_fwd` plus an `A_rev` pass).

### E3, cohort curves
All subsets of k = 2 to 6 participants, for N1, N1-A, N2-D, N4, N4g, N3, S0 and RECSIG.

### E4, within-session: the literature's setting, labelled WITHIN-SESSION everywhere
For each day, using that day's passes only:
- **(a) Temporal split within the natural block:** train on each participant's first 60 % of natural passes and test on the last 40 %.
- **(b) Cross-block within the slot:** train on the natural block and test on the slow and fast blocks of the same day. This is the stricter test, because pace changes.

Run N1, N1-A (with AdaBN statistics from the other participants' same-day blocks), N2, N2-D, N4, N4g, S0 and RECSIG on **identical splits**.

### E5, is within-session the person or the slot?

- **(a) Static-level control on the same splits.** RECSIG accuracy beside every E4 row, with the paired difference (arm minus RECSIG) and its interval.
- **(b) The time-gap test.**
  - For every pair of participants on each day (15 pairs x 2 days), compute the two-class within-session accuracy by restricting each E4 (a) model's posteriors to that pair's two classes.
  - Correlate it with the gap between the pair's natural-block start times (from the handoff table) using Spearman, with a permutation p from shuffled gaps.
  - Do this for N4g and for RECSIG.
- **(c) Empty-room slot recognition.**
  - For each day, split each participant's empty-room recording at the spot, and separately at home, into its first and second halves, then cut 2 s windows.
  - Train on the first halves and test on the second halves, with RECSIG features and with the mean log-spectrum from 4 to 70 Hz per link, both through shrinkage LDA. Chance is 16.67.
  - If an empty room with nobody walking can be told apart by slot, then within-session accuracy cannot be attributed to gait.
- **(d) The RX2 level-removed variant** for N2 in E4, as information on the day 1 low state.

### Statistics
- **Primary:** a 95 % bootstrap over participants, as the frozen scorers compute it.
- **Secondary, shown beside:** a bootstrap over passes.
- **p values:** the permutation p.
- **Always report n.**

## 6. Reading rules (registered now, applied mechanically)

- **R-A, a gait signal across days.** N4g's participant-bootstrap lower bound is above 16.67 in both directions on E1 (ii).
- **R-B, integration adds over pace.** N4 minus the larger of S0 and N3 has a paired participant-bootstrap lower bound above 0 in both directions on E1 (ii). Otherwise N4 is reported as "pace-led".
- **R-C, Wi-Fi-sensed pace.** N3's correlation with press speed is at least 0.7 on the training day, and N3 is within 10 points of S0 on E1 (ii). If both hold, pace is reported as sensed by Wi-Fi rather than taken from the presses.
- **R-D, within-session is the person.** All three must hold on E4 (a):
  - the arm minus RECSIG has a paired lower bound above 0;
  - the time-gap Spearman for N4g is not positive with p below 0.05;
  - empty-room slot recognition at the spot does not exceed the 95th percentile of its permutation null.

  If all three hold, the E4 figure is reported as "within-session identification attributable to the person". Otherwise it is reported as "within-session identification that cannot be separated from slot recognition".
- **Labels.** Every claim carries the label "exploratory, registered before run". No arm replaces a confirmatory row.

## C. Every hyperparameter, and every point the brief leaves open, fixed now

The code is `pipeline/identify/idv2_core.py` (building blocks; every hyperparameter is in its `HP`) and
`pipeline/identify/idv2_run.py` (data, evaluations, reading rules). Frozen functions are imported and
never changed.

### C.1 Data

- **Source.** The addendum E relabelled source, `runs/identify/dc2_src_relabelled/identification/`
  (`idrelabel`), which links every capture file except participant P5's day 2 fast block, rewritten
  with pass 9 closing at its real 5 m press and pass 10 (`A_rev`) excluded. Every walk block of both
  days, both directions: day 1 240 passes, day 2 179.
- **Per pass,** in one fixed order (recording by participant, day and block, then by start press):
  - the frozen PRIMARY feature bank by `id29_dc2_score.extract` (the 150 Hz grid between the presses
    through `id29_dc2_score._link`'s clock map, the 103 subcarriers, `id19_featbank.pass_features`), with
    the feature columns named by Room A's `steady_raw` variant, as `id29_dc2_score` uses them;
  - press speed, 5.0 m over the press interval;
  - the TX-RX2 torso frequency f_t (`id29_dc2_score.torso_hz` on TX-RX2 over the pass) and TX-RX2's
    channel read from the frames;
  - the conditioned maps of section C.2.
- **Empty rooms** are read from the same source.

### C.2 N1, CNN-v2

- **Conditioning, per pass and link, on the pass's 150 Hz grid of 103 subcarriers:**
  - `idmax_features.reduce_link(a, "dop", "pca", n_pc=4)`, which is the 4th-order Butterworth band-pass
    4 to 70 Hz by `sosfiltfilt` over the whole pass, mean removal, the eigendecomposition of the
    subcarrier covariance, the top 4 components, and the positive-skew sign rule;
  - PCs 2 to 4 kept;
  - `scipy.signal.stft(fs=150, nperseg=32, noverlap=24, nfft=64, boundary=None, padded=False)`;
  - the 28 bins from 4.6875 to 67.96875 Hz;
  - |Z| summed over the three PCs, `log(mag + 1e-6)`, minus the mean of that link's whole-pass map.
- **Windows.** Frames [4w, 4w + 34) for w = 0, 1, ..., while inside the pass. Never padded. A pass with
  fewer than 34 frames gives no window and is counted.
- **Normalisation.** For each direction, per link, the mean and SD (plus 1e-6) over every value of
  the training windows of that direction. A direction absent from training (N1-fwd) uses the other
  direction's statistics.
- **Architecture** (19,890 parameters; 19,602 for N1-rx2):
  - Conv2d(3, 16, 3, padding 1), BatchNorm2d, ReLU, MaxPool2d((1, 2));
  - Conv2d(16, 32, 3, padding 1), BatchNorm2d, ReLU, MaxPool2d((1, 2));
  - Conv2d(32, 32, 3, padding 1), BatchNorm2d, ReLU;
  - the mean over time, giving 32 x 28, flattened to 896;
  - concatenated with the one-hot direction (`A_fwd`, `A_rev`), dropout 0.3, then Linear(898, 6).
- **Training.**
  - Adam (lr 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4), 3,000 steps of 64 windows, cosine annealing from 1e-3 to 0
    over the 3,000 steps (stepped each step), cross-entropy.
  - Class-balanced sampling: each of the 64 draws picks a class uniformly among the classes present,
    then a window uniformly within it.
  - Per-sample augmentation, applied to the normalised input in this order:
    - a time mask of width uniform on {0, ..., 4} frames at a uniform start;
    - a frequency mask of width uniform on {0, 1, 2} bins at a uniform start (masked values set to 0);
    - a per-link additive offset ~ N(0, 0.2);
    - each link set to 0 with probability 0.15 (only when there is more than one link);
    - noise 0.02 N(0, 1) on every value.
  - Seeds 0 to 4 (`torch.manual_seed` and a seeded generator for sampling and augmentation), no
    validation split, a fixed budget.
  - CPU with `torch.set_num_threads(4)`, unless the MPS gate of the brief passes at launch; the device
    used is recorded in the results.
- **Prediction.** Per seed, each pass's score is the mean over its windows of the log-softmax,
  renormalised. The pass log-probability is the mean over seeds of those, renormalised
  (log-sum-exp). The decision is the argmax. Each seed's accuracy is reported, with the mean and SD.

### C.3 N1-A

The same trained networks. For each group of test passes, a copy is made in which every BatchNorm
layer's running mean and variance are reset and recomputed with momentum None in ONE forward pass
(the BatchNorm layers in training mode, dropout off, no gradients, no labels) over every window of
that group's "other" passes, normalised with the training statistics. The weights are untouched.

- **E1:** each test block's passes, with the other two blocks of the test day (all participants,
  both directions) as the "other" passes.
- **E4:** each test participant's passes, with the other five participants' passes of all three
  blocks of that day.
- **E2's out-of-fold probabilities:** the held-out block, with the fold's training blocks.

### C.4 N2, N2-D, N3, the integrations and the controls

- **N2.**
  - Features: `FConf("dc2e", "U_all_gait", "mean@b08_35", "all", "pc1-3", "perline_z")`, the columns
    of analysis P1's PRIMARY.
  - Preparation: the frozen `idmax_eval.prep` with per-path z-scoring (`A_fwd` and `A_rev` separately).
  - Head: `idmax_eval.make_model("rf")`, a random forest of 300 trees, `max_features` sqrt,
    `random_state` 0.
- **N2-D.**
  - The training side exactly as N2.
  - Each test pass is standardised per path with the mean and SD of the "other" passes, pooled across
    participants: the other two blocks of the test day in E1; the slow and fast blocks for E4 (a); the
    day's other two blocks for each test block in E4 (b); the fold's training blocks in E2.
  - Those passes are imputed with the training medians, on the same retained columns.
  - A path with fewer than 3 "other" passes uses all of them.
- **N3.**
  - v_hat = f_t x lambda / (2 F), with lambda = c / ((2407 + 5 x channel) MHz) and F = 0.9814, TX-RX2's
    path-mean projected factor at the recorded spot (`id29_dc2_score.link_F`, `id15_factor_map`). The
    same F serves both directions, since F is a magnitude.
  - Classifier: the frozen `idmax_eval.prep` (training-side median imputation of a non-finite v_hat,
    then z-scoring), then shrinkage LDA (`make_model("lda")`: lsqr, automatic shrinkage).
  - The correlation reported is Pearson's r between v_hat and press speed over the training day's
    passes with a finite v_hat.
- **Log-probabilities.** RF and LDA probabilities are floored at 1e-9, logged and renormalised. N4 is
  N1-A + N2-D + N3 and N4g is N1-A + N2-D, summed with weights 1, then renormalised.
- **S0 and RECSIG,** as frozen (`idmax_eval.fit_predict` with `FConf("dc2e_S0", "S0", "-")` and
  `FConf("dc2e", "RECSIG", "mean@b08_35", "all")`, shrinkage LDA), trained on exactly the passes each
  split trains the arms on.
- **The permutation null** is the frozen `id29_dc2_score.id_stats` permutation p (identities permuted
  over participants within the test fold, 1,000 draws).

### C.5 The N1 ablations

- **N1-f.**
  - f_ref is the median f_t of the training passes.
  - Each pass's maps are resampled along frequency so that new(f) = old(f x f_t / f_ref), by linear
    interpolation over the 28 bins, with bins outside the band set to 0 (the map's mean).
  - This happens before windowing and normalisation.
  - A pass with a non-finite f_t is left unscaled.
- **N1-rx2.** The TX-RX2 link only (one input channel), with no link dropout.
- **N1-fwd.** Trained on the training day's `A_fwd` passes only; tested on the same test sets as N1.

### C.6 The evaluations

- **E1.**
  - Test sets: (i) natural `A_fwd`; (ii) every test-day pass.
  - Rows are also given per block and per direction.
  - Every arm and ablation, trained on every pass of the training day.
- **E2.**
  - The temperature is fitted per arm and per direction on out-of-fold log-probabilities of the
    training day. The folds leave one block out (natural, slow, fast), with every arm refitted per
    fold, the CNN with all five seeds.
  - T is taken from `logspace(-1, 1.5, 61)` to minimise the negative log-likelihood.
  - Decisions sum log-probability / T over:
    - each participant's passes in each test-day block;
    - each cycle, meaning an `A_fwd` pass and the next `A_rev` pass of the same block in time (a pass
      without its partner is its own cycle).
  - Arms: N1, N1-A, N2, N2-D, N3, N4, N4g, S0 and RECSIG.
- **E3.**
  - Every subset of k = 2 to 6 participants.
  - Each arm's E1 (ii) log-probabilities are restricted to the subset's columns over the subset's
    passes, with no retraining.
  - The mean, minimum and maximum accuracy over subsets, against chance 100/k.
- **E4, labelled WITHIN-SESSION.**
  - (a) Each participant's natural passes of the day, ordered by start press. The first
    round(0.6 n) are trained on (day 1: 12 of 20; day 2: 6 of 10, both directions) and the rest are
    tested.
  - (b) Trained on the day's natural block and tested on its slow and fast blocks.
  - N1, N1-A, N2, N2-D, N3, N4, N4g, S0 and RECSIG, on identical splits.
- **E5.**
  - (a) Arm minus RECSIG on each E4 row: the per-pass correctness difference with a paired bootstrap
    over participants (`idgp_dc2_score.paired`, 1,000 draws).
  - (b) The time-gap test:
    - for each day and each of the 15 pairs, N4g's and RECSIG's E4 (a) log-probabilities are
      restricted to the pair's two columns over the pair's test passes, giving the two-class accuracy;
    - the gap is the absolute difference, in minutes, between the pair's natural-block `started` times
      in the sidecars;
    - Spearman is taken over the 30 points pooled across the days, with a one-sided permutation p for
      a POSITIVE correlation, the gaps shuffled within each day, 10,000 permutations.
  - (c) Empty-room slot recognition:
    - for each day and each position (spot, home), each participant's empty room is taken on the span
      common to its three links;
    - that span is split at its midpoint, and each half is cut into non-overlapping 2 s windows from
      its start;
    - two feature sets:
      - static levels (`id29_dc2_score.static_levels`: mean amplitude per subcarrier per link, mean
        RSSI per link);
      - the mean log-spectrum per link: Welch with nperseg 64, noverlap 32, nfft 64, detrended
        constant, per subcarrier, the mean over subcarriers, log(power + 1e-12), the 28 bins from 4 to
        70 Hz, 84 values;
    - each feature set is z-scored from the training windows, then classified by shrinkage LDA,
      trained on the first halves and tested on the second (chance 16.67);
    - the null permutes the six recordings' identities on the test side, 1,000 draws; the 95th
      percentile and p are reported.
  - (d) N2 with TX-RX2's amplitude divided, per recording and per subcarrier, by its median over the
    recording, beside N2, on both E4 splits.
- **Statistics.**
  - Primary: the frozen participant bootstrap (`id29_dc2_score.id_stats`, 1,000 draws).
  - Secondary: a percentile bootstrap over passes (1,000 draws).
  - Also the permutation p and n.

### C.7 The reading rules, applied mechanically

- **R-A.** N4g's E1 (ii) participant-bootstrap lower bound is above 16.67 in both directions.
- **R-B.**
  - "The larger of S0 and N3" is the one with the higher E1 (ii) accuracy in that direction (S0 on a
    tie).
  - The difference is N4 minus it, as the paired participant bootstrap of per-pass correctness.
  - It must hold in both directions; otherwise N4 is reported as "pace-led".
- **R-C.** Pearson's r is at least 0.7 on both training days, and |N3 minus S0| is at most 10 points on
  E1 (ii) in both directions.
- **R-D,** on E4 (a):
  - (i) N4g minus RECSIG has a paired lower bound above 0 on each day (the "arm" is N4g);
  - (ii) the pooled time-gap Spearman for N4g is not both positive and at a one-sided p below 0.05;
  - (iii) at the spot, on each day, neither feature set's accuracy exceeds the 95th percentile of its
    null.

### C.8 The figure rule, written before plotting

The per-participant TX-RX2 spectrogram windows show, for each participant and day, one window of one
natural `A_fwd` pass. The pass is the one whose duration is closest to that participant-day's median
natural `A_fwd` duration, the earlier on a tie. The window is its middle one, index floor(n / 2) of
its n windows. It is drawn as the conditioned TX-RX2 map of section C.2 (log magnitude, the pass
mean removed, before the training normalisation), on one colour scale shared by all twelve panels.
