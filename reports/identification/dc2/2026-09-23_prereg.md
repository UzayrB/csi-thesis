# Pre-registration: DC2 identification, confirmatory. 2026-09-23

Written on 23 September 2026, **before any DC2 data exists**. Its SHA-256 is recorded in
`runs/IDENTIFICATION_FROZEN_2026-09-23c.json`, which references the 23b freeze and does not
overwrite it. The scorer, `pipeline/identify/id28_dc2_score.py`, refuses to run if this file's hash
differs from the recorded one. Any change after hashing is therefore visible as a mismatch, and
needs a new, dated pre-registration.

The sheet is `reports/DC2_ID_PROTOCOL_2026-09-23c.md`. Participants are six, reported by code, and
the retired participants and free-movement recordings are never read.

## 1. Data

**Files.** The scorer searches `data/room_B/` recursively for files whose name contains `_idpath_`.
Every such file must match the pattern

`participant_<name>_idpath_d<1|2>_r<1|2|3|4>[_slow|_fast](_RX1.bin|_RX2.bin|_RX3.bin|.gt.json|_run.json)`.

The expected set, for each of the six participants, is eight recordings:

- day 1: r1, r2, r3 and r4_slow;
- day 2: r1, r2, r3 and r4_fast.

Each recording needs all three `.bin` files and the `.gt.json`. The scorer stops before scoring
anything, reporting FAILED and listing every offence, if any of the following holds:

- a file is missing;
- a file does not match the pattern, or is outside the expected set;
- a name is not one of the six, or is retired;
- a recording holds fewer than 3 passes;
- a pass names a path other than E_row1.

**Passes.** Each pass comes from the sidecar's `passes`, as `t_start` and `t_end`. The segment is
the start press to the end press. Amplitude is resampled onto a 150 Hz grid on the receiver clock,
by the id19 method. The fixed subcarrier mask of id19 leaves 103 subcarriers.

**Room A.** Room A passes are used only in analysis c: all 360, or the 18 on E_row1.

## 2. Configurations: frozen, heads fixed, scored once each, never tuned on DC2

- **PRIMARY.** The 23b rule's configuration:
  - features: `id19_featbank.pass_features`, the U_all_gait set on the gait-band mean, all three
    links;
  - preparation: median imputation from the training side, then z-scoring per path from
    training-side statistics (every DC2 pass is on E_row1; in analysis c1 the E_row1 statistics
    come from Room A's training passes on E_row1);
  - head: a random forest, 300 trees, `max_features` sqrt, `random_state` 0.
- **SECONDARY, labelled post hoc.** The 2D CNN on Doppler spectrograms. Its exact recipe is in
  `runs/IDENTIFICATION_FROZEN_2026-09-23b_ADDENDUM.json`: seeds 0 to 4, a five-seed probability
  ensemble, 60 epochs.
- **S0, a control and never a result.** Speed is the path length between the marks (5.0 m for
  E_row1) divided by `t_end - t_start`, and the head is shrinkage LDA.
- **RECSIG, the recording-signature reference.** Static levels only (the mean amplitude per link
  per subcarrier, and the mean RSSI per link) over the segment. All links, shrinkage LDA: the head
  the 23b nested search chose in 43 of its 48 folds.

## 3. Analyses, each run exactly once

- **a. Cross-round within a day.** For each day, train on two natural rounds and test on the
  third, three folds per day. Report per day and pooled over both days.
- **b. Cross-day within DC2.** Train on day 1's three natural rounds and test on day 2's; then the
  reverse. Report each direction and pooled.
- **c. Cross-room.** Test on every DC2 natural-round pass.
  - Row c1 trains on all 360 Room A passes.
  - Row c2 trains on the 18 Room A E_row1 passes.
- **d. The pace test, gait against speed.**
  - **Scoring.** For each day, train on that day's three natural rounds and test on its pace round
    (day 1 slow, day 2 fast). PRIMARY, SECONDARY and S0 are scored on the same passes.
  - **Drop.** For each configuration and each participant-day unit u, the drop is acc_a(u) minus
    acc_d(u):
    - acc_a(u) is accuracy on u's passes in analysis a;
    - acc_d(u) is accuracy on u's pace-round passes;
    - the configuration's drop is the mean over the 12 units, and its paired 95 percent interval is
      a bootstrap over those units, 1000 draws.
  - **Reading, fixed now:**
    - **Manipulation check.** S0's drop interval must lie above zero. If it does not, the pace test
      is NOT DECIDABLE and no gait-against-speed verdict is given.
    - **Not informative.** A configuration whose analysis-a interval contains chance has nothing to
      keep, and is reported that way.
    - **Otherwise, one of three verdicts:**
      - ENCODES PACE: its drop is at least S0's drop, meaning it drops as much as speed-matching
        does;
      - GAIT-CONSISTENT: its drop interval contains zero, and its upper bound is below S0's drop;
      - INCONCLUSIVE: anything else.
- **e. Controls on DC2.**
  - **RECSIG, recording recognition.** RECSIG is run as analysis a and as analysis b. Recording
    recognition counts as SHRUNK by separate rounds if RECSIG's analysis-a upper bound lies below
    86.11, the lower bound of Room A's within-day RECSIG figure (91.67).
  - **SESSION-ID, a day-ID on DC2.** PRIMARY's features and head predict the day (d1 or d2, chance
    50) on the natural rounds. The split is by participant: train on three people, test on the
    other three, all 20 splits.
- **f. ICC.** PRIMARY's per-pass correctness in analysis a is grouped by participant-round
  recording and estimated with the one-way estimator `id15_corridor.estimate_icc`. It is reported
  beside Room A's 0.421; there is no gate.

## 4. Statistics, for every row

Every row reports:

- accuracy and n;
- chance: 16.67 for identity, 50 for the day;
- a 95 percent cluster bootstrap over participant-round recordings, 1000 draws;
- a permutation p over 1000 draws, with labels permuted over recordings.

For identity, the six identities are permuted among the six recordings of each test round. For the
day-ID, the days are permuted among each participant's recordings. The test labels stay sealed
until each fold's predictions are committed. No configuration, head, threshold or rule is chosen
or changed after DC2 data exists.

## 5. Stated expectation, so it cannot be reinterpreted later

At the measured ICC of 0.421, a six-way result has a 95 percent half-width of about 15 points for
analyses a and b, and about 11 points for c. **A true accuracy near 24 percent therefore cannot be
distinguished from chance in a or b.**

**Analyses a, d and e are the ones DC2 can decide.**

- **a:** whether cross-round identification within a day exists once the recording confound is
  broken.
- **d:** whether a classifier reads gait or speed.
- **e:** whether separate rounds shrink recording recognition compared with Room A.
