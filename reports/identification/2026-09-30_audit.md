## 3. Verification: the synthetic walker (for the thesis)

**What it is.** A simulated walker on line A was written in the capture format. Its timing and every
non-CSI field come from a real recording. It passed through the unmodified pipeline:
- reading;
- amplitude;
- the clock map;
- subcarrier selection;
- 150 Hz resampling;
- the gait feature extractor;
- the Doppler map.

**The simulated signal:**
- **paths:** a static path, a torso path and two limb paths, with the torso's path length changing
  at 2 F v(t) (F = 0.98 on RX2);
- **walker:** v rises from rest to 1.2 m/s in about 1 s and oscillates by 15 % at a step rate of
  1.8 Hz;
- **timing:** it reaches 5 m in 4.48 s.

| check | result |
|---|---|
| 1. torso frequency within one FFT bin of 2 F v / lambda | **pass:** 18.75 Hz measured against 17.97 Hz predicted (bin width 2.34 Hz) |
| 2. cadence within 5 % of the true step rate | **pass:** 1.786 against 1.8 Hz |
| 3. no NaN among the model's features | **failed** as built: `dwt_6` on each link. **Pass** after fix B-1 (0 of 630) |
| 4. gain invariance (all amplitudes x2) | features: **pass** (largest relative change 9e-15). Maps: **failed** as built (2.1e-4). **Pass** after fix B-2 (0.0) |
| 5. 2 % frames with a zero HT-LTF half (measurement) | the map's 35-70 Hz over 10-35 Hz energy shifts by +0.94 dB. Stage 1 found no such frame in the data |
| 6. edge transients (measurement) | filtering within the pass instead of on the continuous stream: relative RMS error 1.58 in the first second, 0.10 in the middle and 0.17 in the last second (0.8 to 3.5 Hz band); 0.95 / 0.10 / 0.04 (0.3 to 2 Hz) |

- **Other checks:**
  - payload x2 before int8 rounding changes features by a median 3.5 %, which is the size of int8
    quantisation on a weak synthetic signal;
  - the tests are `tests/test_idz_synth.py` (5 pass).
- **One error in the simulator itself:** its motion grid first ended 1 s after the start. It was fixed
  before any check was read.

## 4. Statements to correct in the thesis

1. **Subcarriers.** "103 subcarriers" should be **103 CSI entries: 54 unique subcarriers**. Of these,
   49 are measured in both the LLTF and HT-LTF fields and 5 in one only. Five valid entries are
   dropped (LLTF +1 and HT-LTF -28 to -25). No kept entry is a null.
2. **The frequency ceiling.**
   - The measured packet rate is **about 149 per second** (median 148.7 to 149.3 per receiver), so the
     Doppler ceiling is **about 74.5 Hz**, not "75 Hz at 150".
   - Four Room A session 1 recordings run at 135 to 140 per second, a ceiling of 68 to 70 Hz.
3. **The torso-frequency agreement** (18.75 Hz measured against 19.5 Hz predicted) holds **at FFT-bin
   resolution**. The bins are 2.34 Hz wide, and the synthetic walker reproduces the same one-bin
   agreement.
4. **Rhythm visibility.** Step or stride rhythm is visible in **about 30 % of natural identification
   walks** on RX2: 30.6 % on the Doppler-band energy envelope, 28.3 % on the gait-band channel. Not
   3 %. The 3 % was measured on the torso-frequency contour, which cannot resolve the step rate.
5. **The identification-walks headline.** 46.67 becomes **43.33 [26.11, 62.78]**, because a wavelet
   feature that was undefined for most walks acted as a pace proxy (bug B-1). The leave-one-session-out
   figures stand: Room A 39.44, Room B 30.00.
6. **Gait feature count:** 630, not 633.
7. **"All features are shapes or ratios, so gain cancels"** holds for the features (to 1e-14) and, after
   B-2, for the maps.
8. **The AGC:** the ESP32 firmware reports no AGC gain (the field is 1.0 throughout). Per-packet
   amplitude dips of at least 3 dB occur in about 2.6 % of packet pairs, equally in empty rooms, and
   are not compensated.

## 5. What went wrong

- **The synthetic walker's first run** cut the walk after 1 s (the motion grid ended too early). It
  was fixed before any check was read.
- **The first packet-rate calculation** printed 5.8 per second for one file. That file's RX2
  rebooted, and the formula counted the counter reset. The reboot itself moves pass timing by at
  most 0.06 ms. The clock map treats a reboot as a counter wrap, a latent robustness issue with no
  effect on this dataset.
- **The bank builder's first grid** was built as start + i/150 instead of the bank's `arange`, a
  difference of 3.5e-8 s that moved some sensitive features by up to 0.017. It was fixed, and the
  builder then reproduces the bank to 3.6e-12.
- **Queue and handover:**
  - the first hold job failed (the priority wrapper cannot run a shell loop), and one training was
    started before the hold;
  - it was stopped by PID at 22:42 so tracking could have the GPU first;
  - tracking then asked for time to write its pre-registration, so identification resumed and
    handed the GPU over at 23:01;
  - it resumed at 23:46.
- **One log entry** lost its component names to shell backtick expansion. It was fixed in f3b1e17.

## 6. Limits

- **Sample size:** six participants and two test days. Paired intervals over participants are about
  ±5 to ±20 points, so the rule cannot establish gains under about 10 points on this data.
- **GS, the most promising candidate:**
  - every component improves, and both test days improve;
  - but its +10 points is not separable from zero here;
  - it also kills the mean-channel features, which suggests those were dominated by per-packet
    amplitude noise rather than gait.
  - A follow-up would need its own pre-declared test (Uzayr's decision).
- **Development evaluation, all data seen:** the ensemble's components were chosen in earlier batches
  with the same walks in view.

## 7. Answers, in plain words

- **Is the pipeline correct?** Mostly yes. The packets are clean, the clock alignment is sound, and the
  torso Doppler and cadence come out right on a simulated walker.
- **Two real bugs were found and fixed:**
  - a wavelet feature that was really a hidden measure of walking speed;
  - a tiny non-invariance in the Doppler maps.
- **What the fixes cost:** 3.3 points on the identification walks (now 43.33 %), and nothing on the
  tracking-walk figures (Room A 39.44 %, Room B 30.00 %).
- **Is anything holding the figure down?** None of the seven candidates cleared the rule:
  - fusion, layout-aware calibration, layout-keyed statistics, continuous filtering and
    frame-normalised maps changed nothing detectable, or hurt fast walks;
  - per-packet amplitude normalisation raised the figure by 10 points and helped every part of the
    model, but six people are too few to prove it.
- **The thesis needs eight corrections,** listed in section 4.
