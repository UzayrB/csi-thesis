## 1. What identification does

From **one key press** when a walk starts, the live app names which of **six people** is walking along the
identification path, from the Wi-Fi channel state (CSI amplitude) of three ESP32 receivers. It never sees the end press,
the walk's duration, tracking's output or any label. Chance is 16.67 %.

**People (names only, never codes):** PA5, PA1, PA2, PA3, PA4, PA6. Identification's old reports call
them P1 to P6 in that order; **tracking numbers people differently (its "P5" is PA2)**, so a code read across the two
branches names the wrong person. New outputs use names.

**The rig:** TX at (2.5, -0.5), RX1 at (2.5, 5.5), RX3 at (5.5, 2.5); RX2 at (2.0, -0.5), beside the TX, for the
identification walks, and at home (-0.5, 2.5) for tracking. The identification path is line A (x = 2.5, y 0 to 5): away
is `A_fwd`, towards is `A_rev`. Channel 11, lambda = 0.123 m. The projected bistatic factor F on line A: RX2 0.981,
RX3 0.50, RX1 0.

**The data (1,019 walks, five sessions):**

| session | room | walks |
|---|---|---|
| A1, A2, A3 | A | 120 tracking walks each (`data/room_A/session_01` to `_03`) |
| B1 | B | 120 tracking (`data/room_B/session_B1`); identification walks 120 natural, 60 slow, 60 fast |
| B2 | B | 120 tracking (`data/room_B/session_B2`); identification walks 60 natural, 60 slow, 59 fast |

The identification captures are in `runs/identify/dc2_src_relabelled/identification/session_01` (B1) and `session_02`
(B2); `pipeline/identify/idx_bank.recordings()` lists every recording. `data/` is never edited.

## 2. Engine v4, the final engine

`pipeline/identify/engine.py` (`ENGINE_VERSION = 4`, `SUFFIX = "_v4"`, `T_S = 4.0`), run live by
`pipeline/live/identify_engine.py`.

1. **The press** sets t0. **The window** is [t0, t0 + 4 s]; a later pending press ends it early.
2. **The causal clock:** each receiver's microsecond clock is mapped to the host clock from its trailing 20 s of packets;
   the decision comes once every receiver has a packet past the window's end, or at t0 + 5 s at the latest.
3. **Direction:** RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy slope on the first 3 s (96.9 % right on 419 identification walks).
4. **The end detector** (radio only): RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy at checkpoints 3.0 to 4.0 s; the walk has ended at the
   first run of 10 frames below alpha times its early level. Alpha is in each model's manifest (0.2 for `loso_B1_v4`,
   0.5 otherwise).
5. **Wi-Fi speed** = the torso contour's median on the best link x lambda / (2F), on the walking span.
6. **Features and maps** on the whole window: 630 gait features (rhythm, Doppler contours, cycle templates, spectral
   shape, wavelets, modulation, cadence) and per-link conditioned maps (4 to 70 Hz band-pass, per-window PCA keeping PCs
   2 to 4, STFT 32/24/64, 28 bins, log minus mean).
7. **Eight components:** three random forests (per-path z; session standardisation; that plus the Wi-Fi speed), extra
   trees and a shrinkage LDA (each with the Wi-Fi speed), the 3-link CNN plain and with AdaBN, the per-link CNNs with
   AdaBN. **Fusion:** the equal-weight sum of log-probabilities; the displayed confidence is softmax(sum / 8).
8. **Same-day calibration:** unlabelled, causal, one pool per antenna layout (fix L); fewer than 10 windows means
   training statistics.

**It carries three bug fixes:** v2's cadence fix (1 Oct), v3's fix L (calibration per layout) and fix F (no rhythm, no
step rate). **The CNN weights** are v3's (trained 30 Sept on 3 s maps), applied to the 4 s maps.

**Models:** `runs/identify/engine/models/<model>_v4/` (`loso_A1_v4` to `loso_B2_v4` give the figures; each trained on the
other four sessions). **`deploy_all5_v4`** (all five sessions, 1,019 walks) is the rig's model; the guard refuses any
model trained on the streamed day. Bank: `runs/identify/engine/bank_T4.0_v4/`. Replays:
`runs/identify/engine/replays/loso_<S>_v4_<S>_T4.0.*`.

## 10. Pitfalls learned

- Hand-built engine inputs must carry their `layout`: without it, `engine.Session` mixes the tracking and
  identification walks in one calibration pool (fix L); two teaching scripts had this fault on 2 Oct.
- Watchers on `guard.log` must read only lines written after they start (an old "QUEUE STOPPED" fired one), and macOS
  `wc -l` pads with spaces (`tail -n +$(wc -l < f)` fails in zsh).
- Tool background commands stop after about 30 minutes; long queues were run detached with `nohup`.
- The names guard refuses outputs that contain `P1` to `P6`; row ids avoid them.
