walk_edge (first to last window START of the pass, as id13 cut it) is 9.0 s median (p10 8.0,

the pass's own steady median:
  3.5 s BEFORE the start press, energy 0.88 to 0.96 of steady: the approach walk and the turn onto

  4.3 s steady walking between the presses: 47.7 percent of the span.
  1.4 s AFTER the end press, energy falling from 1.0 to 0.84: braking and turning.
  2 of 360 spans start within 2 s of the previous pass's end press.
So about half of every walk_edge feature came from acceleration, turning and braking, not steady
gait. The steady span (press to press) is used from here on, with walk_edge kept as a variant.
