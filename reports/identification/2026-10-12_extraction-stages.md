The extraction from the trimmed frames equalled the untrimmed one, bitwise on every field but `extract_s`, on 3 of 20 windows; the JSON records only equal or not. By the code, the field `n_frames` (engine.py line 321) counts every frame in the buffer, so it differs whenever trimming removes frames. A spot check after the run (a one-off script, not kept, 12 Oct about 12:30, on windows 0 and 7 of the first recording, the first two payload windows) found window 0 equal (its buffer was under 21 s old) and window 7 different in `n_frames` only. The other 16 differing windows were not checked field by field.

## 7. Notes

- Every second here is one process alone on an otherwise quiet machine; in the app three workers and the main process share the CPU, so absolute times there can differ (section 1 compares the totals). Shares and the (a)/(b) split are the transferable facts.
- The timers add a small cost per call (a few microseconds); it falls in the caller's self-time, and in `extract_v4`'s own statements.
- This report proposes nothing and the app is unchanged.
