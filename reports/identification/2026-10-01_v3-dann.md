## 1. Part 1: the bug fixes

**What the bank does for a missing step rate** (the brief's question; read in the code before the PLAN note):
- **There is no unscaled fallback.** With no step rate, `rhythm_features` and `cycle_templates` return nothing, and
  `doppler_features` skips its stride-scaled part.
- **Those columns are NaN,** and every component imputes them from its training medians, as any missing feature.
- **How many windows it touches:** after fix F, 71 of the 1,019 windows at T 3.0 have no step rate (65 at T 2.5, 63 at
  T 3.5; none before the fix).
- **What changes:** 688 windows' features, mostly in the per-link step-rate columns, whose resolvers fall back far
  more often than the whole-window one.

**The screen** (`runs/identify/v3/rows/part1.*.json`; paired against engine v2 on the same 180 walks):

## 2. Part 2: DANN at seed 0, both folds

**The promise rule** (quoted from the PLAN note of 18:12):

> **The promise rule (Arm A only), at a lambda_max**, PROMISING if all hold:
> 1. the paired natural gain over the same-seed baseline is at least +4.45 points;
> 2. the paired change is above zero on to B1 and on to B2 separately;
> 3. the walks named PA1 do not rise above the same-seed baseline's.

**How each arm was built** (`pipeline/identify/iv3_dann.py`):
- **Networks:** all four CNNs (the 3-link `cnn3` and the three 1-link networks) were trained for each fold, five
  times: the baseline, Arm A at two values of lambda_max, and Arm B at two. That is 40 trainings, 6,000 steps each.
- **The domain head:** a gradient-reversal layer on the pooled 896 features, then 896 to 64, ReLU, and one output per
  domain. lambda follows the paper's schedule.
- **The domains:** the training sessions, with Room B split by layout (5). Arm B adds the test session, unlabelled, as
  a sixth.
- **The same-seed baseline** is the engine's own trainer at seed 0. It reproduced the engine's saved seed-0 networks
  **bit for bit** (largest parameter difference 0.0 on all eight networks), so seed noise is fully held constant.
- **Scoring:** each arm's seed-0 CNN components, with the v3 reference's feature components and same-layout AdaBN.

**Reading:**
- **Arm A is not PROMISING at either lambda_max:**
  - at 0.1 its gain is 0.00, far below +4.45, and it is not positive on to B1;
  - at 1.0 it loses 5.00 points.
  - So seeds 1 and 2 were not run.

  the CNNs away from the lean, but not towards the right person.
- **At lambda_max 0.1 the head can still tell the sessions apart** (58 to 96 %), so the reversal is too weak to align
  much. **At 1.0 it falls to 32 to 42 %,** 1 to 14 points above its majority share, so the CNN features carry far
  less of the session, and identity gets worse (Arm A), or no better (Arm B).

## 4. Plain statements

- **DANN is not worth keeping.**
  - In its deployable form (Arm A) it did not beat the same seed without it: 0.00 at lambda_max 0.1, -5.00 at 1.0.
  - Its only consistent effect was to name PA1 less often, not to name people correctly.
- **Arm B says that aligning the CNN features across days closes almost none of the cross-day gap.**
  - With the test day's own unlabelled windows in training, the best Arm B moved the result by +1.11 [+0.00, +3.33],
    against a same-day advantage of 20 to 33 points (AC2).
  - When the alignment was strong enough to bring the domain head down to 32 to 39 % (majority shares 29 and 35 %;
    lambda_max 1.0), identity got no better (-1.11).
  - So what separates same-day from cross-day accuracy is not a shift of these features that unsupervised alignment can
    undo. Same-day evaluation is confounded by the recording slot (AC2's own caveat), and cross-day identity is the
    signal that is left.
  - One seed and two folds; information only.
- **Identification closes at engine v3:** 32.22 % [17.78, 48.33] on natural walks, Room A 31.67 %, Room B 27.50 %,
  verified 299 of 299 through the app.
