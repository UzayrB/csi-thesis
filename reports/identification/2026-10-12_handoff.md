## 6. Measurement facts worth knowing

- The decision delay is counted from t0 + 4.0 s and includes the wait for the 0.30 s window and its packets (median 0.33 s);
  the 0.5 s target is the processing after that ("delay minus wait_s").
- A recording's closing-poll decisions (12 on B2) have their packet wait recorded as the cap, so their processing comes out
  negative; quote stream-only counts beside the totals.
- With parallel extraction, the engine's `model_s` includes the wait for the last window's worker; the classification alone is
  `model_s` minus `worker_waited_s`.
- Memory logs store top's MB divided by 1024; multiply by 1024 to get top's own MB back.
- cpu against the mps replay differs by up to 9.47e-06 on closing-poll decisions with any code; the 8.1e-06 bound covers the 287
  decisions made during the stream.
- The GPU-side memory growth (about 1.9 to 2.0 GB/h over a day) is in the main process, serial and parallel alike; it is under
  the redefined limit by only 0.07 GB/h, and its cause is not attributed (the app's own arrays are 0.08 GB per day).
