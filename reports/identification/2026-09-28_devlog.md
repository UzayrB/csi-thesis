The development log that replaces pre-registration for identification, under the operator's direction

## The requirements that still bind (from the direction)

- **No leakage.** Splits are by whole sessions, and every statistic is fitted on training data only,
  or on causal pre-pass data such as a calibration walk.
- **Live claims are causal.**
- **Gait-based.** Pace enters only as one parameter inside gait analysis, never as a bare speed
  comparison; S0 stays a control.
- **Data.** `data/` is read only; the free-movement recordings (Room A session 3's `DEMO_free`) are
  never trained or tested on. Session 3's walks are named `*_TEST_walk` because it was tracking's
  test session; they are ordinary walks, 20 passes per participant, and are used, as Room A's
  identification bank always used them (`idmax_common`).

## The evaluation standard for identification

- **Leave one session out** over A1, A2, A3 (Room A sessions 01 to 03) and B1, B2 (DC2's 26 and 27
  September): train on four, test on the fifth, rotating through all five.
- **Choices on validation only.** Every tuning and model choice is made on validation inside the four
  training sessions (an inner leave-one-session-out), never on the test session.
- **Reported:** six-way accuracy per held-out session and per room, with 95 percent intervals over
  participants and n, beside the frozen references:
  - Room A's nested cross-day headline, 15.83 [10.28, 21.67];
  - DC2 analysis P1, 26.67 [13.33, 36.67];
  - P1-GP, 30.00;
  - analysis P6, Room A to Room B, 10.00;
  - S0, 40.00, a control.

## The first batch: the plan

1. **One pass bank for all five sessions.**
   - Per pass: the conditioned maps (the frozen chain to the STFT, as v2), the frozen PRIMARY
     features, press pace, and the path identity.
   - Built from `data/` read only, through the addendum E relabelled source for B's identification
     captures, and the tracking relabel of 2026-09-28 for B1's tracking walk.
2. **Baselines under the new standard,** to set the bar per held-out session: RF on the PRIMARY
   features; CNN-v2 as registered for v2; S0 as a control only.
3. **Generalisation levers,** each chosen by inner validation:
   - per-session normalisation from causal pre-pass data (the calibration walk, or the session's
     walks before the one scored);
   - direction and path inputs;
   - per-link against joint encoders, and link fusion including product fusion;
   - augmentation for placement change (the brief notes RX2's placement varies between recordings);
   - multi-room training;
   - integration of gait with pace as one gait parameter, never bare pace.
4. **Real time.** The chosen model is run causally, pass by pass, as `pipeline/live/identify.py`
   does, and its latency is reported.
5. **One closing report for the batch.**
