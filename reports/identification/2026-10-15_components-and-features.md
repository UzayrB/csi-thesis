   component, is -1.36 [-4.60, +1.74]; favouring the strongest component costs -12.09 [-23.50, -0.91]. Equal weights

3. **The three feature groups that matter most** (the ensemble's accuracy drop when a group is permuted, the two core
   cells pooled: 539 rows, in which B2's 120 path walks appear twice, once under each core model): the Doppler

4. **They name days rather than people.** On raw features the 630 name the session at 76.79 % (balanced accuracy, chance

## 1. What was run

- **Part 1** re-weighted engine v5's nine components (six feature components: rf, rf_prior, rf_pace_prior, et_pace_prior,
  lda_pace_prior, hgb_pace_prior; three CNN components: cnn, cnn_prior, cnn_perlink_prior) from the stored arrays of
  the 13 v5 replays, with no retraining. Five candidates were fixed before any score. Each candidate's weights for a
  walk of session S were fitted only on the held-out outputs of the other four sessions (the leave-one-session-out
  replays). The headline is v5's own (Z + D) / 2, with the guards (natural, slow, fast, Room A and Room B tracking walks,
  at most 5 points below v5) and the core cells (xr_ABtrk A3+B2 path, xr_A all B2, no fall at all).
- **Part 2** permuted 40 groups of the 630 gait features (and the Wi-Fi speed) through engine v5's own offline feature
  path, 20 repeats each, on five model-sessions: xr_ABtrk on A3 and on B2 (path walks), xr_A on B2 (all walks), and
  loso_B1 and loso_B2 (identification walks). "Core pooled" joins the two core cells: 539 rows, not 539 walks, because
  B2's 120 path walks appear under both core models (xr_ABtrk and xr_A). The CNN components are the stored arrays,
  since a feature permutation cannot reach them. It then asked each family whether it names people or days (2b),
  whether it holds speed (2c), and what the biased importances say (2d, secondary only).
- Intervals: participant bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 0, paired against v5 on the same walks. Chance: 16.67 %.

## 2. Part 1: the nine components

**No candidate passes, and equal weights stand.** Every candidate scores below v5 on the headline, and each also fails
at least one core cell (Table 1). Without the installation rule every candidate is lower again, and v5 itself without
the rule loses 0.88 points [-1.29, -0.35], so the Room A weight earns its place.

**Which component carries the answer: none alone.**
- On all 1,019 held-out walks the per-link CNN is the best single component (29.54 %), but its interval overlaps the
  four forests (26.99 to 28.95 %). Every component is 4 to 10 points below the sum (Table 3).
- **The sum is carried by the sharp components.** The LDA supplies about 0.29 of v5's winning margin, the per-link CNN
  0.23, the boosted trees 0.17 and the other two CNNs 0.10 to 0.12; the four forests supply about 0.02 each. This repeats
  the 1 Oct screen (spreads 10.06 and 9.43 then; 7.73 and 6.37 here at walk level).
- **The best component changes from session to session** (Table 4). By accuracy there are three winners and two changes
  over the five sessions; by log-loss, three winners and three changes. On the pooled held-out walks of the other four
  sessions a forest always has the lowest log-loss (the forests are the best calibrated), so BEST1 picks a forest every
  time, and a forest alone is a weak voter.
- **The two families are wrong on different walks** (Table 6). Between a feature component and a CNN component the
  error correlation is a median 0.062 (largest 0.114), against 0.419 within the feature components and 0.445 within the
  CNNs. That independence is what lifts the equal sum above every part.

**Log-loss is the wrong target for these weights.** The fitted temperatures flatten the LDA 8 to 11 times, the CNNs and
the boosted trees 2 to 6 times, and sharpen the forests 1.3 to 2.2 times (Table 2). STACK puts most of its weight on
the forests. Both are fitted for calibration, and both lose accuracy. Even fitted on the scored cell itself (an
unattainable oracle, Table 5), stacking by log-loss stays below v5 on both core cells (30.00 against 32.08, and 29.43
against 32.11).

**Leaving one component out** never moves v5 by more than 1.47 points on the held-out walks. One leave-out has an
interval above zero: without cnn_prior, +0.88 [+0.10, +2.25]. It is one of nine diagnostics, not a fixed candidate,
and it does not hold across sessions (A3 +5.00, A1 -2.50, the zero-shot natural cell -5.00), so it is not a lead.

## 3. Part 2: what the 630 features use

**The offline path is exact.** On the unpermuted bank it reproduces the stored replays' feature components, every
window, at a maximum absolute difference of 0.0 on all five model-sessions (section 5). Each unit's baseline was asserted
equal again before its permutations. The ensemble baselines are v5's own: core pooled 32.10 %, natural 36.67 %.

**2a. The Doppler contours carry the feature side** (Tables 7 to 9, Figure).
- Permuting the Doppler contours costs the ensemble 7.00 points [+3.94, +11.15] on the core cells pooled. The cost is
  separable on both core cells, on the natural walks (+4.89) and on the slow walks (+7.00). It spreads over the links
  (RX1 +3.71, RX2 +2.87, RX3 +3.03), so no single receiver carries it. Inside the ensemble, it costs each feature
  component 7.6 to 9.1 points except rf (2.12).
- **Modulation** (12 columns: modulation depth, RSSI spread and subcarrier dispersion per link) has the second-largest
  point estimate (+4.81), but its interval crosses zero and swings with the cell (xr_A all B2 +6.86 [-3.99, +18.73]). It
  names the day (raw session 72.38 %) and not the person (16.58 %), and its day-within-person share is the largest of
  any family (0.266).
- **Wavelets** +2.40 [-0.21, +5.25] are not separable. **Cycle templates** (+1.34 [+0.78, +1.92]) and **cadence**
  (+0.85 [+0.39, +1.47]) are small but separable on the core cells. Cadence turns slightly the other way on the natural
  walks (-1.31 [-2.31, -0.39]).
- **Rhythm**, the largest family (219 columns), adds nothing measurable (+0.49 [-0.22, +1.22]), although the forests'
  impurity importances give it about a quarter of their splits (Table 14), which is the bias those measures are known for.
- **All families on one link:** RX1 +3.22, RX2 +6.25 and RX3 +3.44 points on the core cells, each separable. RX2's
  interval is the widest ([+0.92, +11.55]).

**2b. People or days** (Tables 12 and 13).
- **On raw features every family names the day better than the person.** All 630: session 76.79 %, day within Room A
  83.06 %, day within Room B 89.14 %, against the person across days 25.41 %. The Doppler contours, modulation and
  wavelets name the session at 59.34 %, 72.38 % and 45.29 %. Rhythm, cycle templates, spectral shape and cadence name
  neither well.
- **Per column**, the share of variance between participants is a median 0.006 against 0.027 for day within person,
  and the between-participant share is the larger in only 10 of 630 columns. (Day within person includes each day's
  shift common to everyone.)
- **After the engine's per-session standardisation** the day is no longer nameable (session 23.95 %, chance 20). Day
  within Room A falls to 11.39 %, below its chance of 33.33, which a leave-one-participant-out split can produce once
  each session is centred on its own walks; read it as no recoverable day signature. The person across days rises only
  to 27.96 %, and the Doppler contours are the best single family for it (25.82 %).
- **Unexplained, flagged:** cadence (6 columns) names the day better after standardisation than before (session 37.97 %
  against 21.64 %).
- This supports the thesis's explanation, "the limit is the day", feature by feature: the features vary more from day to
  day than from person to person, and what remains once the day is removed names people only weakly.

**2c. Speed in disguise** (Tables 10 and 11).
- The Methodology's claim that the 630 are speed-free by design holds for six families. Their columns correlate with
  neither the Wi-Fi speed nor the press-implied speed (no column above |r| 0.3 on the pooled walks), and no ridge
  regression fitted leaving one session out recovers either speed from them.
- **The Doppler contours are the exception:** leaving one session out, they predict the press-implied speed (R^2 0.378;
  on the pooled walks largest |r| 0.475, 9 columns above 0.3) but not the Wi-Fi speed (R^2 -0.135), although on the
  pooled walks 2 of their columns pass |r| 0.3 against the Wi-Fi speed (largest 0.349). Across sessions, then, they
  carry the press-implied speed and not the Wi-Fi speed. The two speeds themselves correlate at only r = 0.407 on
  these walks.
- Permuting the Wi-Fi speed together with a family changes its drop very little: Doppler contours 7.00 alone, 7.37 with
  the speed. The Wi-Fi speed column alone costs +0.58.
- So part of the Doppler contours' value may be how fast people walked (as the presses measure it), and this test
  cannot separate that from gait shape.

**2d. Secondary only** (Table 14; biased measures, never leading a conclusion). The forests' impurity importances and the
LDA's coefficients put the Doppler contours and rhythm first (about a quarter each) and give modulation (12 columns)
several times its share of the columns. Only the Doppler contours' lead agrees with 2a; rhythm's does not.

**2e** (CNN frequency-band and link masking) was optional and not run; it needs its own slot on a free machine.

## 4. What this means for the Discussion

- **Equal weights are the right call, now with a reason measured on the held-out walks.** The ensemble works because two
  families with nearly independent errors vote together. Re-weighting by calibration moves weight to the best-calibrated
  but weakest voters, and favouring one component fails because the best one changes from day to day.
- **The feature side's identity information sits mostly in the Doppler contours.** The same family also carries the
  walking speed (as the presses measure it) and the day, so its value cannot be called pure gait.
- **"The limit is the day" holds feature by feature:** no family names people across days well (at best 22.87 % raw),
  every family varies more between days than between people, and calibration removes the day without uncovering much
  person.
