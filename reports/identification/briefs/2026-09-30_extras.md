## 2. Stage A: a bounded attempt at a gain for the one-press engine

**The principle every candidate must respect** (Uzayr, 30 September):
- The engine learns only from prior sessions, so the test day contributes no labels and no protocol.
- No warm-up walk by each person and no per-day enrolment.
- A candidate may change what the engine learns from its training days. It may not add anything taken from the day it is tested on.

**Where to look first.** The engine's natural confusion shows **63 of 180 walks predicted as P2, against 30 expected**. P3's, P5's and P1's walks go there most (14, 12 and 9). Its top-2 is 54.44 against a top-1 of 36.11, so the right person is often second. So the largest lever is the model's lean towards one person. The only fix allowed is one learned from the training days (A4).

**Report every candidate, adopted or not.**

### 2.1 How candidates are judged (the audit's rule, reused)

**The baseline.** The current engine, scored by engine replay with its current causal calibration, on natural identification walks pooled over "to B2" (`loso_B2` replaying B2) and "to B1".

**Order.** Test candidates in the fixed order below. Each is compared with the current best configuration, which includes anything adopted before it, on the same walks, paired, with a 95 % bootstrap over participants (10,000 resamples).

**Adopt a candidate only if all of these hold:**
1. the paired gain on natural identification walks has an interval **entirely above zero**;
2. slow and fast walks each lose no more than 3 points (point estimate);
3. leave one session out on the 600 tracking passes loses no more than 3 points pooled (point estimate).

**Report for every candidate:**
- natural (to B2, to B1 and pooled), away, towards, slow and fast;
- the paired difference and its interval;
- the decision.

**Cost.** A1 to A3 change only the five feature components' inputs; the three CNNs are untouched. So they can be scored by recomputing the feature components' outputs under the same causal calibration and adding the saved CNN outputs, without retraining any CNN. Say how, and verify the adopted configuration by a full engine replay at the end (§2.3).

### 2.2 The candidates, in stacking order

**A1: precise Wi-Fi speed** (the physics gate first, then identity).
- **The problem.** Today's Wi-Fi speed takes about 14 distinct values, because the torso frequency is read in 2.34 Hz bins. Its correlation with press speed on natural walks is only 0.60. Press speed alone (S0) reaches 38.33, which shows how much precise speed is worth among six people.
- **The new estimator.**
  1. Per frame, compute the power-weighted centroid of RX2's conditioned spectrogram between 10 and 35 Hz, after subtracting the empty-room spectrum at the identification spot. Use only frames above the window's 25th energy percentile.
  2. v = median over frames of f_centroid × 0.123 / (2 × 0.98).
  3. Off line A, keep the current best-link rule with its F, and NaN below F = 0.3.

  The empty-room spectrum is a fixed property of the node position, not of any participant.
- **Identity-blind gate.** Proceed to the identity test only if the natural-walk Pearson correlation with press speed rises from 0.60 to at least 0.70.
- **The candidate.** The new speed replaces the pace column in the three pace components.

**A2: speed-profile ("gait initiation") features.** Every walk starts from standing, and how a person gets up to speed is a gait trait.
- **The trace.** From the per-frame speed trace of A1 (the new estimator even if A1 is not adopted), take:
  - the speed at 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5 and 3.0 s, as the median within ±0.25 s;
  - the time to reach 90 % of the window's maximum;
  - the maximum;
  - the distance covered (the integral).

  That is 9 columns, NaN where the best link's F is below 0.3.
- **The candidate.** Append these 9 columns to all five feature components' inputs.

**A3: more training rows from offset windows.**
- **The candidate.** For training only, add feature-component rows from windows starting at t0 + 0.15 s and t0 + 0.30 s, still 3 s long, for every training pass. That gives three rows per pass, with the same labels.
- **Test rows.** Unchanged: the window at t0.
- **Calibration and standardisation** use only the t0 rows, as now.

**A4: correcting the lean towards one person, learned only from the training days.**
- **The idea.** If the model systematically over-names some people when it meets a day it has not seen, that tendency can be measured on the training days themselves. Hold each training session out in turn, and see who the model over-names on it.
- **How, for each outer fold** (for example "to B2", whose training sessions are A1, A2, A3 and B1):
  1. **Inner leave-one-session-out.** Train the full engine on three of the four training sessions and replay the fourth with causal calibration, exactly as the outer evaluation does. Repeat for all four.
  2. **The bias vector.** For each person c, m_c is the mean ensemble posterior for c over all inner held-out walks (identification and tracking), divided by 1/6.
  3. **The correction.** On the test day, log p′_c = log p_c − log m_c, then renormalise.
  4. **Nothing is taken from the test day.** The vector is fixed before the test day is replayed.
- **For the deployment model,** the vector comes from the five existing leave-one-session-out models on their own held-out sessions. That is already the inner loop for a model trained on all five.
- **Also report:**
  - the bias vectors per fold;
  - whether the training-day lean matches the test-day lean (the same people over-named or not);
  - each person's share of the predictions before and after;
  - how often the corrected answer was the uncorrected runner-up.
- **Cost.** 4 inner trainings per outer fold.
  - Score it on the two identification-walk folds first: 8 trainings, about 2 hours on the GPU.
  - Only if it is adopted, add the inner trainings for the three Room A folds (12 more) so the tracking-pass figures come from the same procedure.

  If tracking holds the GPU for long, run A4 last and say so.

## 5. The rig run: recorded B2 through the real rig path

### 5.1 The recorded-stream source

A source that stands in for the three serial ports:
- **The source files:** it reads B2's `.bin` files per receiver (identification recordings through the relabelled source, as `ide_replay` does).
- **Release:** each receiver's raw frames are released in recorded order, at their original `t_mono` timing, through the same interface the serial reader gives the app. The recorded `t_mono` already carries the serial batching, so the timing is reproduced.
- **Presses:** only the recorded start press of each pass is injected, as a space-bar event at its recorded time. End presses and metre anchors are never injected.
- **What it streams:** exactly the recordings, order and presses that the harness used for B2, so the rig run can reproduce the thesis figures.
- **Command:** `python3 -m pipeline.live.identify_engine rig --source recorded --session B2 [--speed 1|max] [--from <recording>]`.
- **The serial path is unchanged.** `--source serial` is the default, and its code is not modified.

### 5.2 The model guard

- **The rule:** a recorded stream must use a model that never saw the streamed session.
- **For B2,** the app loads the final `loso_B2`, trained on A1, A2, A3 and B1 only.
- **The guard:** the app reads the model's manifest and refuses any model whose training sessions include the streamed session. That includes the deployment model, which stays the default for real boards.
- **On screen:** the model's name and "RECORDED STREAM: B2".

### 5.3 The time base

- **The requirement:** the engine takes every time it uses from the stream's own time base (the recorded `t_mono` and the injected press times), never from the host's wall clock. That includes the window, the packet wait, the 1 s cap and the calibration order.
- **The test:** decisions at `--speed 1` equal decisions at `--speed max`, pass for pass, on at least one full B2 recording.
- **If the test fails,** fix only the time base, record the change and its reason, and re-run every §5.5 test.

### 5.4 Calibration from earlier in the day

- **A whole-day stream** calibrates naturally, exactly as the harness did.
- **Starting at a later recording:** `--from <recording>` first feeds every earlier B2 recording at maximum speed with no display, so calibration is identical to the whole-day stream. Only then does it play from the chosen recording at `--speed 1`.
- **The test:** decisions after `--from` equal the whole-day stream's decisions for the same passes.

### 5.5 Tests (add to `tests/`)

1. **The rig run equals the harness.** The rig run with the recorded source and the final `loso_B2` gives the same decisions as the harness's B2 decisions, pass for pass.
2. **Speed does not matter.** `--speed 1` equals `--speed max` (§5.3).
3. **Starting later does not matter.** `--from` equals the whole-day stream (§5.4).
4. **The boards would still work.** A fake serial port object delivering the same frames through the serial code path gives the same decisions as the recorded source.
5. **The guard works.** It refuses the deployment model for a B2 stream.
6. **The overlay never changes a decision.** Decisions are identical with the §6 overlay on and off.
