## 0. Uzayr's vision, which governs every choice in this brief

**One identification model, and it is the live app.**
- **One press.** The operator presses the space bar once, when the walker starts on line A. That press is the signal to start identifying; nothing else is pressed.
- **A fast answer.** The model answers on its own, about 1 s after it has heard enough.
- **The same model offline.** Every thesis figure comes from replaying recorded sessions through this same engine, with the same code, the same causal timing and the same model. There is no separate offline model and no second pipeline that could disagree with the live one.
- **Renders.** Renders are made by replaying the recorded data through the live app and screen-recording it.

So the engine is built once, as a library:
- the evaluation harness and the live app both call it;
- the training data are extracted by that same engine code, so training, evaluation and live inputs cannot diverge.

**Fixed facts.** No further data will ever be collected. DC1 (A1 to A3) and DC2 (B1, B2) are the whole dataset.

## 4. Stage 2: the one-press engine

### 4.1 What the engine does, from the press to the answer

1. **Stream.**
   - The engine receives each receiver's frames as they arrive.
   - It keeps a rolling buffer.
   - It keeps a **causal clock map** per receiver (§4.2).
2. **Press.**
   - The space bar sets t0.
   - The engine's window is **[t0, t0 + T]** with **T = 3.0 s**, fixed in `PLAN.md`: about 3.5 m of a natural walk, and shorter than about 95 % of natural walks. This replaces the end press.
3. **Wait only for packets.**
   - The engine decides once every receiver has delivered frames past t0 + T, measured on the causal clock.
   - It also decides at t0 + T + 1.0 s at the latest, with whatever has arrived.
4. **Extract.**
   - Features and Doppler maps are computed from the window with the final signal path (B1, plus GS if adopted).
   - The code is the same code that built the training bank (§4.4).
5. **Direction** is detected from the signal (§4.3). It gives the path, `A_fwd` or `A_rev`, which per-path statistics and the CNN direction flag need.
6. **Speed** is measured by Wi-Fi, not by presses (§4.3). It replaces press-timed pace as the single pace column in the three pace components.
7. **Calibrate causally.** Use every earlier window in the session, unlabelled, with the same thresholds as now (at least 10 windows for feature standardisation, at least 1 for AdaBN). This is the only calibration a live system can do, so it is the headline calibration.
8. **Decide.**
   - The eight components are fused by the equal-weight sum of log-probabilities.
   - The answer is the argmax.
   - The displayed confidence is the geometric mean of the eight: the sum divided by 8, then normalised. It never changes the decision.

### 4.2 The causal clock map

- **The offset.** Per receiver, it is the 5th percentile of (t_mono − local time) over the frames in the trailing **20 s**, updated as frames arrive, with at least 20 frames; otherwise use t_mono.
- **Wraps and reboots.** Unwrap as today. Treat a large backwards jump in local time as a reboot and restart the estimate. This fixes the latent issue the audit found.
- **Identity-blind check before any training.** Across every recording, compare the causal map with the frozen centred map. Report the time difference: median, 99th percentile and maximum.

### 4.3 Direction and speed from the signal (identity-blind, rules fixed in `PLAN.md`)

**Direction.**
- **The rule:** the sign of the slope of RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy over the window. Falling means walking away (energy is strongest near the TX); rising means walking towards.
- **Check:** report its accuracy on every identification walk.
- **Fallback:** if accuracy is below 95 %, the live app uses two trigger keys, one per direction, which is still a single press. Report which one was used.

**Wi-Fi speed.**
- **Formula:** v = f_torso × lambda / (2 F), where:
  - f_torso is the window's median torso-contour frequency;
  - lambda = 0.123 m;
  - F is the known mean bistatic factor of the best link for that path and layout (from `id15_factor_map`).
- **On identification walks,** that link is RX2 beside the TX, with F = 0.98.
- **Missing values:** a pass whose best F is below 0.3 gets NaN, which is imputed.
- **Check:** report the correlation and median absolute error against press speed on the identification walks. This is a physics result for the thesis.

Press speed (S0) remains only as a control computed from the recorded presses, never as an input.

### 4.4 One code path for training, evaluation and live

- **Build the training bank by running the engine's own extraction on the recordings.**
  - Replay each recording's frames in arrival order through the causal clock.
  - Take every recorded pass's start press as t0.
  - Cut [t0, t0 + T].
  - Label each window with the pass's participant, and with its true path for training.

  Tracking passes are included for training, as in TR-AB. For them, the window is the first 3 s of the walk.
- **Retrain the frozen eight-component ensemble on this bank.** The components, features, CNN settings and seeds stay the same.
- **Evaluate only by replaying recordings through the engine,** in recording order. Use the press times as the only input and ignore every other press. Decisions are made causally, exactly as live.
  - A test verifies that the evaluation harness and the live app produce identical decisions on the same replay.
  - They must, because they are the same code.

### 4.5 What to score

All figures come from engine replays, with the final signal path and causal calibration.

1. **Identification walks.** TR-AB training, "to B2" and "to B1", pooled. Report:
   - natural, away, towards, slow and fast;
   - S0 and the paired difference against it;
   - the engine without its three pace components;
   - per participant, with the confusion matrix;
   - top-2, round trip, 3 and 5 walks, and groups of 2 to 6.

   CAL-others is reported as information only.
2. **Leave one session out** on the tracking passes, 5 folds, Room A and Room B.
3. **The cost of the vision, for information and never used to decide.** Report the paired difference between the one-press engine and the two-press configuration (B1, or B1+GS). If the one-press engine loses more than 5 points on natural identification walks, say so prominently. The design stays one-press; that is Uzayr's decision.
4. **T sensitivity, information only.** Run T = 2.5 and 3.5 s on the identification walks. The engine keeps T = 3.0, because switching after seeing the test days would be selection.
5. **Latency.** The distribution in replay of the decision time after t0 + T, and after the true end of the walk (from the recorded end press, for reporting only).

## 5. Stage 3: the live app is the engine

1. **Identification mode.** `pipeline/live` gets an identification mode that wraps the engine and nothing else.
   - **Rig:** three receivers over serial, one space bar press per walk.
   - **Replay:** recorded `.bin` files, played in real time or faster. Each recorded pass's start press is replayed as the single space bar press, and every other press is ignored.
   - Both feed the same engine object.
2. **Models.**
   - **Deployment model.** Train it on all five sessions with the engine bank, for the real rig. Save it with a manifest recording the configuration, commits, data and the GS decision. It is never used to score anything.
   - **Held-out models for renders.** In replay mode the app loads, by default, the fold model that never saw the replayed day:
     - the "to B2" model for B2 recordings;
     - the "to B1" model for B1.

     So a screen-recorded render shows genuine decisions on an unseen day. The screen names the model in use.
3. **Screen, per walk:**
   - the detected direction;
   - the top identity with its displayed confidence, and the runner-up;
   - chance (1 in 6);
   - Wi-Fi speed;
   - latency;
   - the status "calibrating: n walks so far" while fewer than 10 windows are in the session;
   - "closed set: six enrolled people";
   - in replay, "REPLAY".
4. **Round trip.** When a towards walk follows an away walk within 15 s, also show the round-trip decision (summed log-probabilities), labelled as a round trip.
5. **Walker tally (optional).** A key marks "new walker". Until the next press of it, show the sum over that walker's walks, labelled "k walks". It marks a change of walker only, never who the walker is.
6. **Handoff to tracking.** Write each decision as one JSON line under `runs/live/`, with the time, direction, top identity, probability, runner-up and round-trip or tally decision. Document the format in `reports/ID_LIVE_INTERFACE.md`. Never edit tracking's code.
7. **Render command.** One command that replays a chosen recorded session through the app in real time for screen recording, for example `python3 -m pipeline.live replay-identify --session B2 --realtime`.
8. **Tests.**
   - An app replay equals the evaluation harness's decisions pass for pass.
   - The deployment model loads from its manifest.
   - One press produces exactly one decision.
   - The legacy random-forest mode still gives its old decisions.
