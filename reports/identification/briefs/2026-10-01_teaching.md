## 2. Part 2: the teaching pack

### 2.1 Choose the two worked walks by a rule written down before looking

Before reading any walk-level output, append a dated note to `runs/identify/engine/PLAN.md` with the rule below, and commit it.

**The rule:**
1. **The test session is B2**, replayed by `loso_B2` (trained on A1, A2, A3 and B1 only), with causal calibration. This is the deployment-realistic fold and the render's day.
2. **The confusion.** Among B2's natural identification walks, take the commonest confusion: the off-diagonal cell with the most walks.
   - Ties go to the cell with the larger true-person row total, then to the true person who appears first in recording order.
   - That cell's true person is the worked person.
3. **The recording.** Choose the first recording, in recording order, holding at least one walk by that person predicted as the confused person (wrong) and at least one predicted correctly (right). Prefer a recording where both are in the same direction; take the first such recording if one exists, otherwise the first recording satisfying the rest.
4. **The wrong walk** is the first walk of that confusion in the recording.
5. **The right walk** is the person's correct walk in that recording whose displayed confidence is closest to the median displayed confidence of all correct B2 natural walks. Use the same direction as the wrong walk if possible. This gives a typical right walk, not the best.
6. **Record which branch of the rule decided each choice.**

If the rule cannot be satisfied (no such recording), say so and fall back, in order, to the next confusion cell. Record the fallback.

**A. Per worked walk (both):**
1. **Identity and context.**
   - Name, session, recording, pass index, direction (true and detected), pace block.
   - The press time (the only input besides radio).

4. **Conditioning, step by step, before and after,** per link, downsampled to keep the size down:

   2. band-pass 4 to 70 Hz;

### 2.4 Three checks the page's chapter 09 needs (new measurements; the engine does not change)

Before running any of these, write the definitions in the dated `PLAN.md` note. Report every result as it comes out, including results that weaken the story.

**1. The substitution test (swap in another person's radio; the answer should follow it).**
- **Setup:** B2 natural identification walks with `loso_B2`, using the replay harness. For each host walk, splice in the radio of a donor walk:
  - the donor is the next walk in recording order by a different person, in the same direction, at natural pace, with the pairing fixed by this rule before running;
  - copy the donor's frames for all three receivers from donor press − 1 s to donor press + 4 s, time-shifted onto the host's press;
  - the host's press, calibration pool and everything else stay unchanged.
- **Report:**
  - the fraction of swapped decisions equal to the donor's own decision in its own slot;
  - accuracy against the donor's true identity and against the host's;
  - the same with the calibration pool rebuilt as if the donor's walk had happened in that slot.
- **The two worked walks:** include each one's swapped decision in the pack, with its donor named.

**2. The shuffled-label control.**
- Retrain the full engine for the "to B2" fold with participant labels permuted within each training session, keeping each session's class balance. Use one permutation with a fixed seed: one full training, about 16 minutes on the GPU.
- Also run 20 further permutations of the five feature components only, which is cheap, as a null distribution.
- Replay B2 natural walks and report accuracy against chance. The 20 feature-only permutations give an empirical p-value for the real feature components.

**3. The conditioning ablation** (the spec asks the page to show conditioning was vital, so measure it).
- Retrain only the three CNN components for both identification folds on maps built without one conditioning step at a time:
  1. no band-pass;
  2. PC1 kept (PCs 1 to 3);
  3. the subcarrier mean instead of PCA;
  4. no log or mean subtraction (raw STFT magnitude).
- Re-score the ensemble with each variant's CNN outputs and the frozen feature components.
- Also report, identity-blind, the RX2 torso band's energy over the empty room before and after conditioning.

That is about 8 CNN-only trainings. These are **information only and never adopted.** If conditioning turns out not to matter much, the pack says so plainly, and the page will say so too.
