## 3. Definitions (into `PLAN.md` before any training)

**Scoring set.** Room B's identification captures only, from the dev batch's bank with the relabel manifest applied:
- participant P5's day 2 fast `A_fwd` pass 9 closes on the real 5 m press;
- participant P5's day 2 `A_rev` pass 10 is excluded.

Before anything else, print and save a count table: day × participant × direction × pace. Stop and report if any cell is empty or differs from what the protocol implies.

**Directions.** Confirm from the capture metadata and the tape coordinates that `A_fwd` walks away from the TX (from 0 m near the TX to 5 m) and `A_rev` walks towards it. Reports say "away" and "towards", with the path name beside each.

**Round trip.** An `A_fwd` pass together with the `A_rev` pass that immediately follows it, in the same recording, by the same person, paired by recording order. List any unpaired passes. They are left out of the round-trip measures only.

**Folds.** There are two, one per test day.
- **To B2:** train on data from before B2, test on B2. This is the deployment-realistic fold, because the training days precede the test day, so report it first.
- **To B1:** train with B2, test on B1.

The main figure pools both folds.

**Training sets.** The test day's data is never in training.

| name | contents |
|---|---|
| TR-B | the other Room B day's identification captures only (all paces, both directions): Uzayr's "train on B1, test on B2" |
| TR-B+ | the other Room B day's identification captures plus its tracking passes |
| TR-AB | A1, A2 and A3 plus the other Room B day, everything (the dev batch's training data) |

**Calibration.** This is the unlabelled test-time adaptation (AdaBN statistics and session standardisation).
- **CAL-causal:** as in the dev batch, the test day's earlier recordings in recording order.
- **CAL-others:** for each test pass, every recording of the test day by the other five participants. Labels are never used to fit anything. They are used only to leave out the target person's own recordings. This is what a household deployment could rely on.
- **CAL-none:** no test-day adaptation. Components that adapt use their training-set statistics.

**Measures.**
- **Headline:** six-way top-1 on natural-pace passes, both directions pooled, CAL-others.
- **Beside it:** CAL-causal and CAL-none.
- **Also reported:** slow and fast walks, away and towards, per participant, and a confusion matrix.

**Statistics.**
- 95 % intervals by bootstrap over participants (10,000 resamples), paired whenever two settings score the same passes.
- Chance is 16.67 % unless a measure states its own.

**Controls beside every headline row:**
- S0: pace from the presses alone, an LDA on that one number, trained on the same training set's identification captures;
- the ensemble without its three pace components.

## 4. Stage A: which training data (questions 1, 2, 4)

   - Save every trained component under `runs/identify/idwalks/models/<set>_<fold>/`.
   - Later stages reuse these models and never retrain them.
2. **Score.** Score every identification pass of the test day (all paces, both directions) under each of the three calibration settings. This is inference only.
3. **Save outputs.** Save per-pass log-probabilities for every component and for the ensemble.

**Table.**
- **Rows:** training set × calibration.
- **Columns:** to B2, to B1, pooled.
- **Blocks:** natural (away, towards, both), slow, fast.
- **Controls:** the two from §3 beside it.

**Decision rule for the headline training set.** Take the simplest set, in the order TR-B, then TR-B+, then TR-AB, that is not clearly beaten by a larger set. "Clearly" means that the paired difference on natural walks, pooled over both folds, has a 95 % interval entirely above zero. Report all three sets whatever the rule picks.

**Calibration check (question 4).**

## 5. Stage B: which direction (question 3)

1. **Pooled-direction models (from stage A, headline training set, CAL-others).**
   - Compare away against towards, paired by round trip (same person, same trip), on natural-pace passes, per fold and pooled.
   - Repeat for TR-B if it is not the headline set.
2. **Direction-specific training.**
   - Train once on away-only identification captures and once on towards-only, under TR-B and under TR-AB. In TR-AB the tracking passes stay unchanged; only the identification captures are restricted by direction.
   - That is 2 directions × 2 sets × 2 folds, 8 trainings.
   - Score each model on both directions:
     - **the same direction:** a one-way corridor;
     - **the other direction:** does identity learned one way carry over?
3. **Rule.** Declare a direction "better" only if the paired pooled interval in step 1 excludes zero. Otherwise write "no detectable difference at this sample size", with the point estimates, the per-participant breakdown and the number of pairs.
4. **Physics beside it (identity-blind).** For each direction:
   - RX2's torso-contour frequency against the bistatic prediction;
   - the §7 band energies.

   Amplitude-only CSI cannot see the sign of the Doppler, so the torso frequency should not depend on direction. Any direction difference in accuracy must therefore come from something else:
   - which side of the body faces the link;
   - where along the walk the strongest signal falls:
     - walking away, the walker speeds up near the TX and walks steadily far from it;
     - walking towards, the steady walk gets closer and the signal is strongest at the end.

   Say which explanation the data supports, or that they cannot tell.

## 6. Stage C: deployment measures (from saved log-probabilities, no retraining)

Compute these for the headline configuration, and also for TR-B and CAL-others if that is not the headline. Give each measure's chance beside it.

- **Single walk:** top-1 and top-2 (chance 16.67 and 33.33).
- **Round trip:** away plus towards, log-probabilities summed.
- **Several walks:** groups of k = 3 and k = 5 consecutive same-day natural passes by the same person, non-overlapping, any direction, log-probabilities summed.
- **Group size:** 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 people. Use every subset, take the argmax over that subset's classes only, and average over subsets. Chance is 1 over the group size.

These measures sit beside the single-walk figure. They never replace it.

## 7. Stage D: can the ESP32 see arms and legs? (identity-blind, light, can run while waiting)

1. **Band energies.** For each link (RX1, RX2 beside the TX, RX3), each direction and each natural walk, take the median conditioned spectral energy in three bands, relative to the empty room at the same node positions:
   - 10 to 35 Hz: torso;
   - 35 to 55 Hz: arms and shins;
   - 55 to 75 Hz: feet;
   - 75 Hz is the ceiling at 150 packets per second.

   Use the RX2-beside-TX empty-room recordings. Check that they exist; if not, use the quietest segment before each walk and say so. Report the ratio and the fraction of passes above 2.
2. **Predicted positions.** Give where each body part should appear, from f = 2Fv/λ with λ = 0.123 m, each pass's pace and the link's F.
   - State these assumptions as assumptions: arms reach about 1.5 to 2 times walking speed, and feet about 2.5 to 3.5 times.
   - Note that anything above 75 Hz folds back into the band.
3. **Cadence.** Report the fraction of natural passes in which RX2's torso contour shows a clear stride periodicity: an autocorrelation peak between 0.8 and 1.3 s, with a threshold fixed in `PLAN.md`.
4. **Figure.** One mean conditioned spectrogram per direction on RX2, with the predicted torso, arm and foot bands overlaid and the 75 Hz ceiling marked.

**Gated exploratory arm (the only model change allowed).**
- **Gate:** the 35 to 75 Hz ratio exceeds 2 in at least half of RX2's natural passes.
- **If the gate passes:**
  - add one feature group (band energy and contour statistics for 35 to 55 Hz and 55 to 75 Hz) to the three random-forest components only;
  - retrain the headline configuration (2 trainings);
  - report it labelled EXPLORATORY beside the frozen ensemble.
- **If the gate fails:** write plainly that the ESP32 data contain no usable limb Doppler at this packet rate. That is the thesis finding.

## 8. Stage E: start press only (for the live demo, question 5)

Re-score the natural identification walks with the end press hidden, using the saved headline models.

- **The end detector.**
  - After the start press, the end is the first time after 1.5 s at which RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy stays below a fraction f of the walk-so-far median for a hold time h. The search is capped at 8 s.
  - f and h come from a small grid written in `PLAN.md`, tuned on the training day only by end-time error. Never tune them on the test day.
- **Pace.** Pace becomes 5 m over the detected duration. All features are computed from the detected window.
- **Tracking.** The detector uses identification's own signals only, never tracking's outputs.
- **Report:**
  - the end-time error against the real press (median and 90th percentile);
  - the paired accuracy change against two presses;
  - the decision latency.
- **Rule.** Recommend start-only for the demo if the pooled paired change has a point estimate no worse than −3 points and an interval that includes zero. Otherwise keep both presses.
