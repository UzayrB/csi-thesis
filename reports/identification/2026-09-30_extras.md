## 1. Section 2: a bounded attempt at a gain

**The rule** (the audit's; each candidate against the current best, in the fixed order). Adopt only if:
1. the paired natural gain has an interval entirely above zero;
2. slow and fast each lose at most 3 points;
3. the 600 tracking passes lose at most 3 pooled.

**How A1 to A3 were scored without retraining any CNN:**
- the five feature components are retrained for all five folds on the candidate's inputs;
- each replayed session's windows are re-scored in replay order, with every earlier window as
  calibration (the engine's own arithmetic);
- the replay's saved CNN outputs are added.

**The anchor, before any candidate:** it reproduced every replay's feature components and ensembles to
5e-14, with 1,019 of 1,019 decisions equal, both with the saved models and with retrained ones. With
nothing adopted, no confirming full replay was needed (brief 2.3).

**A1, the physics result.** The new estimator:
- takes the RX2 10 to 35 Hz spectrum minus the empty-room spectrum, which comes from the training days'
  recordings only;
- uses the power-weighted centroid per frame, median over the loud frames;
- is continuous (179 distinct values on 180 natural walks; the old one had 14);
- has a median error of 0.067 m/s (was 0.083);
- correlates at 0.697 with press speed on natural walks (was 0.604) and at 0.81 on all 419 identification
  walks.

It misses the declared 0.70 gate by 0.003, so it was not tested for identity.

**A2** changes almost nothing: fast walks are identical, and natural walks change by 0.00 pooled.

**A3 hurts:** -3.33, with the interval entirely below zero, and fast walks lose 3.36.

**A4, learned from the training days only:**
- **The inner loop:** 7 inner trainings (one set shared by both folds) and 8 inner replays, every window's
  features equal to the bank.

## 4. The rig run: recorded B2 through the real rig path

**The source** (`pipeline/live/identify_recorded.py`) stands in for the three serial ports:
- **Frames:** it reads B2's `.bin` files per receiver (identification recordings through the relabelled
  source, as the harness does) and releases each frame as a `pipeline.io.parse.CsiRecord`, the object the
  serial reader gives the app.
- **Order and timing:** one global order by recorded `t_mono`, which already carries the serial
  batching.
- **Presses:** only each walk's recorded start press is injected, as a space-bar `KeyEvent` at its
  recorded time. End presses and metre anchors never are.
- **Routes:** tracking recordings carry their known routes with the stream, as the thesis figures did.
- **Ticks:** the app's consumer is ticked after each arrival batch with the stream time.
- **The serial path** (`--source serial`, the default) is unchanged.

**The guard.**
- **The rule:** a recorded stream refuses any model trained on the streamed session. The training sessions
  come from the model's manifest, or from its `train_rows.npy` and bank.
- **For B2:** it loads `loso_B2` (trained on A1, A2, A3, B1) and refuses `deploy_all5`.
- **On screen:** "RECORDED STREAM: B2" and "model: loso_B2 (trained on A1, A2, A3, B1)".

**The time base (brief 5.3): a fault, found and fixed.**
- **The fault:** the consumer polled the engine with the host's `time.monotonic()` instead of the tick's
  time.
  - On the boards these are the same clock (`run_capture` ticks with it).
  - On a recorded stream they are not: the host clock was 1,095,943 s against the stream's 830,307 s.
- **The effect:** every window was decided at its first tick, from almost no frames. **0 of 10** decisions
  on B2's first recording matched the harness.
- **Why the speed test could not see it:** the error is the same at every replay speed; only the
  comparison with the harness exposed it.
- **The fix:** the tick polls with its own time. It is recorded in the PLAN; the engine itself is
  untouched. Every test below was run after the fix.

**Tests** (`tests/test_ide_rigrun.py`, all pass):

| test | result |
|---|---|
| 1. the rig run equals the harness | **299 of 299** B2 decisions, pass for pass (the same press times, argmax, ensembles to 1e-6), in 12 min |
| 2. speed does not matter | `--speed 1` equals `--speed max` on B2's first full recording (10 walks, 97 s, identical ensembles) |
| 3. starting later does not matter | `--from` the second recording equals the whole-day stream for its 10 walks |
| 4. the boards would still work | each recorded frame re-encoded as the firmware's base64 `CSI_DATA` row, read by the real `SerialReader` from a fake port and parsed by the real `parse_line`: identical decisions to the recorded source (first B2 recording) |
| 5. the guard | refuses `deploy_all5` for B2, and accepts `loso_B2` |
| 6. the overlay | decisions identical with the truth overlay on and off |
| no warm-up | the first walk of a fresh session gets a decision (from training statistics; 0 walks seen) |

## 5. The render

**The declared stretch** (in the PLAN before any viewing):
- B2's six natural identification blocks, in recording order, at real speed;
- every other B2 recording before and between them is fed at maximum speed without display.

It is exactly the engine's "to B2" natural figure, so the running score must end at **41.67 % (25 of 60)**.

**The check:** a headless dry run at maximum speed ended at **correct 25 of 60 (41.7 %)**, as required
(`runs/live/identify/render_check_B2/`, 60 stills).

**What Uzayr runs:**

```
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.live.identify_engine rig --source recorded --session B2 --render natural --speed 1 --device mps
```

- **Duration:** about 17 minutes, of which 9.8 minutes are the shown walks (60 walks, 10 per person). The
  window holds its last frame during the fast feeds between blocks, a few minutes in all. The render
  stops after the last natural block.
- **The window:** 12 x 6.6 inches, 1,200 x 660 points.
- **The screen per walk:**
  - the names;
  - the detected direction;
  - the top identity and runner-up with their displayed confidence against the chance line;
  - "truth: <name>" in green or red;
  - "correct k of n (x %), chance 16.7 %";
  - Wi-Fi speed and latency;
  - the model and its training days;
  - "adapting to this room: n walks seen".
- **Stills for the thesis:** add `--snapshots runs/live/identify/render_B2` to save one still after each
  decision. `--headless` with `--speed max` saves the stills with no window in about 10 minutes.

## 7. Statements for the thesis (updated)

**The figures.**
1. **The headline is unchanged:** a bounded attempt with four candidates and a rule fixed in advance found
   no gain. It stays **36.11 % [24.44, 52.22]** on natural identification walks, and leave one session
   out Room A 30.83 %, Room B 29.17 %.
2. **Out of the box** (no same-day adaptation): 32.78 %. Unsupervised adaptation to the day adds about 3
   points, not separable from zero.
3. **Beyond the speed it measures:** the engine beats its own Wi-Fi-speed-alone control by +12.2 points
   [0.6, 25.0]. The press-speed control S0 (38.33 %) uses a stopwatch the engine never has.
4. **Confidence:** 43 % on the half of walks it is most sure of and 46 % on the top 30 %, but "confident"
   mostly means PA1 (AUROC 0.60).

**The system.**
5. **Precise Wi-Fi speed** (spectral centroid after empty-room subtraction): r = 0.70 on natural walks and
   0.81 on all identification walks, with a median error of 0.067 m/s. It is continuous rather than
   binned, but it did not meet its declared gate as an identity input.
6. **The rig run:** the live app, fed recorded B2 through its rig interface as if live, reproduces all 299
   thesis decisions. This holds at real or maximum speed, from any starting recording, and through the
   firmware's serial format and parser.
7. **No warm-up:** the app decides from the first walk; it adapts to the room without labels from walks
   that happen anyway.
8. **The lean towards one person** (63 of the 180 natural walks are named PA1) cannot be corrected from
   the training days: the training-day lean does not reliably predict the test day's (it matches for "to
   B1", not for "to B2"), and the correction moves 9 of 659 decisions.

## 8. What went wrong

- **The time-base fault** in the app's consumer (section 4). It was found by the rig run's first check and
  fixed as brief 5.3 prescribes.
- **The first anchor run stopped:** it tried to load data the running build had not yet written. The load
  was made lazy; nothing was computed on partial data.
- **A gap caught before the data build ran:** the A-stage data builder at first stored no torso contour
  for identification windows, which A3 would have needed if A1 were not adopted. It was added before the
  build.
- **The render first continued past its last shown block,** feeding hidden recordings to no purpose. It
  now stops after the last shown block; the score is unaffected.
- **The app's help text** still said participants are shown as codes. It was updated.
