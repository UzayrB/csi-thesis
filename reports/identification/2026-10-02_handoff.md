## The final engine

- **Engine v4:** `pipeline/identify/engine.py` (`ENGINE_VERSION = 4`, `SUFFIX = "_v4"`, `T_S = 4.0`), the live app's
  model (`pipeline/live/identify_engine.py`).
- **What it does:** one start press; the window [t0, t0 + 4 s]; the direction from RX2's 10 to 35 Hz energy slope on the
  first 3 s; a radio-only end detector (alpha per model, in its manifest); the Wi-Fi speed on the walking span; eight
  components (three forests, extra trees, a shrinkage LDA, three CNN components) fused by an equal sum; causal same-day
  calibration per antenna layout (fix L). It carries engine v2's cadence fix and v3's fixes L and F.
- **Models:** `runs/identify/engine/models/<model>_v4/` (`loso_A1` to `loso_B2` for the figures; `deploy_all5_v4`,
  trained on all five sessions, 1,019 walks, for the rig). Bank: `runs/identify/engine/bank_T4.0_v4/`.
