## 1. The dot: K1g adopted

### 1.1 The decision and its status

**K1g:**
- **The look-ahead:** the drawn path after the lock reads 1 window past each step, within the locked line.
- **The glide:** over the anchor span (press + 2.0 s to the first decoded frame), the dot glides from the
  locked line's entry to the first decoded frame's position, instead of holding at the entry.

**It is now the app's display:** `final_model.DISPLAY_CANDIDATE = "K1g"`.

**How it was chosen: Uzayr's decision on development data,** the rotation figures in
`runs/dc2_results/display/DOT_CHOICE.json`.
- **The pre-registered rule** (`rule/DOT_PREREG.md`) had kept K0, because every candidate raised the share
  of frames with the dot ahead of the truth.
- **The record:** the decision is recorded as such in `runs/dc2_results/display/DOT_DECISION.md`.
- **A3 and B2:** no candidate had been replayed on either before it.

**On the rotations** (correct-lock walks, every frame from the press; weighted B1 x2, A2, A1):
- **The error:** K1g 0.292 m against K0's 0.351 m, 17 % lower.
- **The share ahead:** 0.326 against 0.314, 1.2 points higher.
- **Unchanged:** every lock and committed line.
- **Causal:** the minimum margin is 0.500 s. Every frame reads radio that ended at least 0.5 s before it is
  shown.

- **The render rules** (from the logs alone):

## 2. The first press: no change. The first walk's lock delay

**What is kept:** the app keeps its first press as it is (the decision on `LIVE_APP_BRIEF2` section 4).

**Why the first walk's lock is late:**
- **What the first press costs:** at a session's first press the app re-cuts the windows the stream retains
  (about 20 s) on that press's phase, fixes the recording's statistics, runs AdaBN, and runs the five heads on
  the walk's windows.
- **How long it takes:** that costs 0.9 to 3.2 s of service time. Later presses cost about 0.9 s.
- **The consequence:** so the first walk's lock appears late on a live screen.

**Measured in the streamed B2 run** (wall clock, the lock frame against its due time, press + 2.0 s):

| recording | the first walk's lock | every later walk's lock: median / maximum |
|---|---|---|
| PA5 | 0.71 s late | 0.13 / 0.17 s |
| PA1 | 1.01 s late | 0.13 / 0.17 s |
| PA2 | 0.97 s late | 0.13 / 0.17 s |
| PA3 | 1.31 s late | 0.12 / 0.16 s |
| PA4 | 0.97 s late | 0.13 / 0.16 s |
| PA6 | 2.13 s late | 0.13 / 0.16 s |

**So on a live screen:**
- **The first walk of a session:** its lock appears 0.7 to 2.1 s after its due time (about 1 s typically).
- **Every later walk:** 0.12 to 0.17 s, the window's cutting delay.
- **Unaffected:** the logged answers.

This is the streamed run's measurement on the K0 display. K1g's look-ahead changes no press's work.

## 4. A fault found and fixed: a K1g replay overwrote a K0 record

**What happened:**
- **The trigger:** the teaching pack's replay passes no display, so it drew with the app's display, now K1g.
- **Where it wrote:** into the untagged folder `runs/live/replay/final/semi_causal/`, which holds the K0
  replays of commit `63987d1`.
- **The damage:** it overwrote B2 PA2's K0 log and summary there. The decisions were unchanged; the dots
  and timings changed.

**The fix:**
- **The records:**
  - the K1g log was moved to `runs/live/replay/final/semi_disp-K1g_causal/`;
  - the K0 files were restored from `63987d1`;
  - `git status` shows `semi_causal/` unchanged.
- **The code:**
  - `app_replay.replay_inner` now tags the folder with the display whenever it is not K0, even when that
    display is the app's default, so the untagged folders stay K0's;
  - the returned summary gives the log's path;
  - the two readers that hard-coded the untagged folder now read that path: the stream's re-replay
    (`stream_source.rereplay`) and the identity check (`tests/_identity_isolation_run.py`).
- **The tests:** the live tests pass (30 passed, 1 skipped: stream, rig, tracking, decision display, truth
  isolation).
