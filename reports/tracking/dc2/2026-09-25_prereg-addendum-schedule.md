## The schedule

- **Order.** Each day, participants come in whatever order suits them. No protocol step and no tool
  requires an order between people. `capture_idboth --plan-day` still prints an order, and that is
  information only. The capture tools never enforced one (checked in the code on 2026-09-25).
- **The calibration walk** is walked by whoever comes first that day, recorded as `<their name>_C` in
  that day's session. Its manifest entry names that recording.
- **P codes** (P1 PA5, P2 PA1, P3 PA2, P4 PA3, P5 PA4, P6 PA6) are fixed labels, not
  slots.

## What is unchanged

- **Within one person:** identification natural first, then the pace order by P code (odd codes slow
  first). The capture enforces this. It fixes the order of one person's blocks, not who goes when.
- **The block per person:** addendum 2026-09-25's sequence.
- **Every pre-registered test, P4 included.** P4 classifies which person's recording a window came
  from, not which slot of the day.
- **Tracking:** its pre-registered outcomes pool passes and are unaffected. Any per-person or within-day
  tracking figure was already disclosed as confounded by the block and still is.

## The cost, disclosed

Day 2 reversed day 1 so that time of day and room conditions could not line up with identity across
days. A free schedule gives up that protection by design.

In its place, disclosure. Beside every cross-day identification figure, the report gives each person's
day 1 and day 2 block start times (their sidecars record them) and the gap between them. No figure is
corrected for it. Where the schedule allows it, a person is not recorded at about the same time on both
days. That is a preference, not a rule.
