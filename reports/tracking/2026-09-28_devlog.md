## D1. Per-recording normalisation, choices on inner validation (started 2026-09-28)

**What changes:** every recording, training and test alike, is standardised per receiver link and
input channel (CSI shape, RSSI) by the statistics of its own idle radio before its first press. That
is `u1_adapt`'s anchor, and causal pre-pass data.

**Why:**
- **Placement shifts:** DC2 showed per-link shifts between days (3.4 and 4.6 dB of mean RSSI on two
  links), and RX2 is re-placed for every participant. Normalising each recording by its own idle
  radio removes such level shifts before the network sees them.
- **It fixes item 4 (iii)'s mismatch:** that version failed because its test statistics (idle) and
  training statistics (walking) came from different kinds of window. Here both sides use the same
  kind.

**How the choices are made:**
- There are ten distinct three-session training sets. Each is trained under both normalisations and
  scored on the two sessions it leaves out. For each held-out session, the four three-session sets
  inside its training four supply the validation scores.
- **The choices:** normalisation (pooled or per recording), and the lock method (the display lock or
  the scorer lock at w 0.25).
- **The criterion:** the mean of committed and lock accuracy under configuration A, pooled over the
  validation passes.
- The chosen configuration is then trained on the four sessions and tested on the fifth.

**Launched** 2026-09-28 22:20 as one queue of 45 cells (`runs/dc2_results/logs/queue_d1.log`), one training
job at a time:
- the five per-recording finals first, so the held-out table exists early;
- then the 40 inner cells (18 trainings; the two existing pooled three-session models are reused).

**The statistics** are in `runs/dc2_results/prn_stats.json`: 32 recordings, 17 to 159 idle windows
each. They show Room B's CSI feature spread 4 to 10 times smaller than Room A's (PA5: 0.12 to

## D2. Input and training robustness (code built 2026-09-28 22:59; screen after D1)

**What:**
- `pipeline/constrained/pd_train.py` trains one link's network under options. With every option off
  it is `nets.fit_posterior` / `fit_position` bit for bit (`tests/test_pd_train.py`, 5 passed on CPU,
  including "the early-stopped weights equal the recipe's at that epoch"). Option randomness comes
  from a separate generator, so the batch order never changes.
- **aug:** per batch on the raw link input:
  - an RSSI offset uniform ±5 dB (converted by the median idle RSSI spread when inputs are normalised
    per recording);
  - CSI-shape scale log-uniform ×0.25 to ×1.5;
  - a time shift of ±0.25 s with edges replicated;
  - then Gaussian noise, sd 0.1, in standardised units.
- **rrel:** each recording's RSSI minus its own idle-before-first-press mean per link, at training
  and at test (causal pre-pass data). This is subsumed by per-recording normalisation, so it is
  screened only on the pooled base.
- **early:** early stopping on the rotation's validation session (calibration walks excluded), at
  most 40 epochs; the best epoch's weights are kept. The final model, which has no validation
  session, uses the median best epoch. NOTE: in the screen the epoch is chosen on the session that is
  also scored, so this screen gain is optimistic. The confirmation will use a cross-rotation epoch
  (the median of the other two rotations' best epochs) so that its gain is honest.
- **mixup:** alpha 0.2, each sample paired with one from a different training session. The loss is
  the lambda-weighted loss against both targets.

**The screen cells** (`pd_cells`, `d2p_*` on the pooled base, `d2r_*` on the per-recording base):
- only the 0.5 s g25 head is trained;
- the half position head is copied from the D1 base cell byte for byte, since the decode reads
  positions only through the readers;
- the readers stay the pooled D1 rotation cell's;
- the decode uses a one-head pool;
- `*_base_*` shares the D1 cell's weights, so it needs no training.

**Added 23:10:** the honest early-stopping screen, `d2{p|r}_earlyx_*`. The early cells save every

## D3. Decoder and decision (code built 2026-09-28 23:05; runs on D2's networks)

**What:** `pipeline/constrained/pd_decode.py` re-runs a finished cell's decode and both locks
(configuration A) under variants, with no retraining. The variants:
- `ref`, the plumbing check: it must equal the cell's own rows. **EXACT on d1sp_0102_to_B1:**
  120/120 committed and 120/120 locks, in 2.6 min, because the substitution decode is skipped.
- `w<x>`: link fusion by product at w. The null offset is scaled by the recording's own
  fused-to-mean emission-spread ratio, read on its idle windows before the first press
  (label-free and causal).
- `cnull`: the running 75th percentile of the peaks received so far, which is causal.
- `onhead<b>`: a logistic regression on the frozen 64-d embeddings, the three links concatenated,
  trained on walk windows against idle and walk_edge windows of the training sessions. It adds
  -b x logit(p_on) to the causal null, so at p_on = 0.5 it equals `cnull`.
- `relgate`: the gate energy scaled by G0 / g_rec, where g_rec is the recording's median idle band
  energy before its first press and G0 is that statistic's median over the training recordings.
- `abstain`: b_abstain_0.25 in place of the veto.
- `pace`: the pace mean and spread fitted on the cell's training passes.
- Constant overrides: `off`, `margin`, `a2w`, `sd`.

`pd_rule` reads a variant as `<prefix>@<variant>`, and `--arm-lock scorer` compares the lock methods.

**Plan:** `cnull` is adopted whatever it costs (the brief: required for the live claim). Every other
option is layered on it and decided by the rule; then the constants are tuned on the rotations.

**FINDING (23:25), the causal null costs nothing, by construction.** The pooled emission is
renormalised per window so that the best state scores exactly 0 (`g2_multihead.pool`:
`m - m.max(axis=1)`). Every window's peak is therefore 0.000, and the "whole-recording 75th
percentile" reference is identically 0 in every recording (checked on all seven B1 recordings of
d1sp_0102_to_B1). The shipped null level is already causal: a fixed -7.5 nats below the window's best
state, plus the still-gate term. `cnull` gives 0 of 120 committed answers and 0 of 120 locks changed.
Two further observations:
- **The offset is live.** Committed on this cell is 33.33 at offset 0, 37.50 at -7.5 and 35.83 at
  -15, so re-tuning the offset is a real lever.
- **The display lock does not read the null.** It is 18.33 at all three offsets.

The on-a-line head is the null variant that can change anything: it replaces the constant with
per-window evidence.
