Closed 2026-09-29 05:57 SAST. **Decision: ADOPT augmentation + relative RSSI.** Early stopping and
mixup are not adopted.

## Method

- **Base:** D1's winner, pooled normalisation.
- **Screen:** each option alone, with the single 0.5 s g25 head, on the three rotations. Each
  option's cell trains only that head; the position head and the start-of-walk readers stay the
  pooled cell's.
- **The best combination is screened next,** then the winner is confirmed with the full five-head
  pool against the full-pool base `d1sp_*`.
- **Decisions** follow the brief's rule: the objective is the mean of committed + lock with B1 x2, the
  paired interval must clear zero, and the edge guard must hold. All results are configuration A,
  with the shipped decode.
- **Early stopping** is judged by its HONEST form (`earlyx`). The epoch is taken from the OTHER two
  rotations' choices, because the optimistic form picks it on the session it is scored on.

## Reading

- **Each option helps on its own but moves an edge line.** Together, aug + rrel repair each other's
  edge losses: every lock edge line rises in the screen. The pairing makes sense. rrel removes a
  recording's RSSI level; aug teaches the network not to rely on level or on CSI scale.
- **With the full pool the gains fall within Room A** (A2 lock +17.5, A1 +15). The zero-shot Room B
  rotation is flat under the shipped decode. D3's relative gate is what lifts Room B.
- **Early stopping's apparent value was mostly selection on the scored session.** The optimistic and
  honest forms differ by 5 to 14 objective points. It is not adopted.
- **The edge guard at small counts.** Three single options with intervals well above zero were
  rejected on 1- to 4-walk changes in one edge line (base 2 to 4 of 18). The rule was applied as
  written; this is flagged for Uzayr at the midpoint.
