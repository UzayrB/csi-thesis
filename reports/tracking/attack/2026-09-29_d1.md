# Tracking attack, stage D1: per-recording normalisation. Closing report

Brief: `reports/BRIEF_tracking_attack_2026-09-28.md`. Log: `reports/DEVLOG_TRACKING_2026-09-28.md`, D1.
Closed 2026-09-29 01:13 SAST. **Decision: KEEP BASE (pooled normalisation). Per-recording
normalisation is not adopted.**

## What was tested

Every recording, for training and for test alike, was standardised per receiver link and input
channel (CSI shape, RSSI) by its own idle radio before its first press (`prn_stats.json`, 32
recordings, 17 to 159 idle windows each). The start-of-walk readers stayed the pooled cell's, so only
the tracking heads changed. The full five-head pool was trained per rotation, and A3 and B2 were not
touched.

## Reading

Per-recording normalisation is worse on every rotation, most of all within Room A (-19 committed on
A2, -13 on A1). The pre-press idle statistics evidently remove level information the heads use, or
normalise by a noisy denominator: idle windows number as few as 17, and the idle RSSI spread runs
from 0.08 to 3 dB. It does not repair the zero-shot Room B rotation either (-4 committed). The pooled
normalisation is the base for D2.

## What else D1 established (baseline on the split)

The pooled five-head pool on the rotations is the development reference every later stage is
measured against: objective 76.04 (B1 37.50 / 18.33, A2 73.33 / 45.00, A1 45.83 / 28.33).
