## 2. What changed, and why (development only: rotations A1+A2->B1 x2, A1+B1->A2, A2+B1->A1)

Every choice was made by the brief's rule on the three rotations. A3 and B2 were never touched until
06:39. The ladder uses the objective (the weighted mean of committed + lock) and the display lock:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0 pooled baseline (the recipe) | 76.04 | 37.50 / 18.33 | 73.33 / 45.00 | 45.83 / 28.33 | 72/50/72/33 | 50/33/28/22 |

Paired gains (points, 95%): D2 +9.79 [3.54, 16.25]; D3 +13.54 [8.75, 18.55] (scorer lock). Edges are
E_col1 / E_col5 / E_row1 / E_row5, pooled over the rotations.

**D1, per-recording normalisation: rejected.** -17.71 [-26.06, -9.17], and it failed the edge guard.
Normalising each recording by its own idle radio removes signal the network uses.
(`reports/ATTACK_D1_CLOSING_2026-09-29.md`)

**D2, training: augmentation + relative RSSI adopted.** (`reports/ATTACK_D2_CLOSING_2026-09-29.md`)
- **Augmentation:** a per-link RSSI offset of +-5 dB, a CSI-shape scale of x0.25 to x1.5, a time shift
  of +-0.25 s, and mild noise.
- **Relative RSSI:** each recording's RSSI minus its own idle mean before the first press, which is
  causal.
- **Alone, each helped but moved one edge line** by 1 or 2 walks, so the guard rejected each.
  Together they repair each other's edges (screen +22.71 [14.38, 30.62]).
- **Early stopping: not adopted.** Its apparent gain was mostly the epoch being chosen on the scored
  session. The honest version, which takes the epoch from the other rotations, is worse
  (90.42 -> 76.46 on the combination).
- **Mixup: not adopted.**

**D3, decoder: the relative motion gate adopted.** Everything else was rejected on D2's networks:
- link fusion at w 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0;
- lock-only fusion: +6.88, but it fails the edge guard on all four lock edge lines;
- the on-a-line head;
- abstain in place of the veto: +3.33 [-0.00, 7.08], just short of the rule;
- the pace re-fit;
- the null offset, margin, A2 weight and display spread re-tunes;
- the scorer lock against the display lock: -0.21.

**The gate fix, in one paragraph.** The motion gate calls a window "still" when its 0.3 to 2 Hz band
energy is below 0.431, a threshold tuned in Room A. Room B's radio is 2 to 80 times quieter at rest
(RX placement, the room), so Room B walkers were called still and the decoder was forbidden to put
them on a line. The relative gate multiplies each recording's gate energy by max(1, G0 / g_rec):
- g_rec is the median band energy of that recording's own idle radio before its first press, which
  is causal and needs no calibration walk;
- G0 is the same statistic over the Room A training recordings.

A quiet room is lifted into the range the threshold was tuned for; a loud one is left alone. Two
earlier versions were measured and discarded on the rotations, because each scaled Room A recordings
down:
- G0 over ALL training recordings mixed the rooms;
- scaling down as well as up lost a further 15 committed and 18 lock passes on A2.

**The causal null: identical by construction, no cost.** The pooled emission is renormalised so each
window's best state scores 0. The "whole-recording percentile" reference was therefore always 0, and
the shipped null was already causal.

## 4. Configuration B on B2 (the calibration walk)

**B equals A exactly on B2** (87.50 / 47.50). The two parts of configuration B no longer act:
- **the gate:** the relative gate does the gate's re-anchoring already, from pre-press idle radio;
- **the null:** the calibration walk's re-anchoring lands 3.35 nats from the absolute level. That is
  outside DC2's 3-nat guard, so it falls back to the absolute value.

**The calibration walk therefore adds nothing to this model.** That is a result: the system needs
only the idle seconds before the first press. Against the calibrated split baseline (65.83 / 30.00),
B2 is +21.67 [12.50, 30.83] committed and +17.50 [6.67, 26.67] lock.

## 5. Decisions for you

1. **The edge guard at small counts.** Several options with intervals well above zero were rejected
   because one lock edge line moved by 1 or 2 walks from a base of 2 to 4 walks out of 18. An example
   is relative RSSI alone: +15.42 [7.08, 23.12], rejected for E_row5 going 2 -> 0 of 18. It did not
   change the outcome here, because the combination passed. It will govern D4 and D5, though. Options:
   (a) keep the guard literally, as now;
   (b) fire it only when an edge line loses at least 3 walks.
   My recommendation is (a) until FINAL. It is conservative and it is what the brief says.
2. **Configuration B in the final report.** Options:
   (a) report B = A, with the explanation above;
   (b) also decode DC2's own B definition once at FINAL, for comparability with the 65.83 / 30.00
       baseline. That definition replaces the relative gate with the calibration-bracket gate, so it
       is likely worse.
   I recommend (a) as the headline, with (b) beside it if you want the like-for-like number.
3. **A3's small dip.** -5.00 committed [-10.83, 0.00] and -2.50 lock. It is within noise and is not
   something the rules let me tune on A3. The A3 locks' edge lines improved (E_col1 17 -> 50,
   E_row1 33 -> 67), while E_col5 and E_row5 fell from 100 to 67. I propose no action. The
   alternative, adopting nothing that costs Room A anything, would forfeit the B2 gain.
4. **The lock method.** The display lock stays; the scorer lock is reported beside it (A3 65.83,
   B2 51.67).
