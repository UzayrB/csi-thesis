# Tracking attack, stage D5: spatial resolution. Closing report

Brief: `reports/BRIEF_tracking_attack_2026-09-28.md`. Closed 2026-09-29 08:58 SAST. **Decision: neither
option is adopted.** The brief: "Adopt only if line accuracy does not fall."

## What was tested (decode only, in `pd_decode`)

- **g100n:** the 100-cell (0.5 m) head in the emissions at its NATIVE resolution, scaled by the
  100-cell training marginal, instead of summed into 25 cells.
- **interp:** every head's scaled posterior interpolated bilinearly, in probability, at each decoder
  state's exact position between cell centres. This is a probability-weighted within-cell position
  in place of the 1 m staircase.

## Result, on D4's winner (d4c_fuse, relative motion gate, display lock; base objective 122.50)

| option | objective | paired [95%] | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| g100n | 123.33 | +0.83 [-1.46, 2.92] | keep: no gain |
| interp | 118.75 | -3.75 [-7.51, 0.00] | keep: line accuracy falls |

The preview on the per-link networks agreed: g100n +0.83 [-1.67, 3.12], interp -5.42 [-10.83, -0.62].
Resolution moved the wrong way or not at all. Median along-line error on the true line (B1 / A2 / A1
rotations): relgateU 0.43 / 0.37 / 0.33 m, +g100n 0.50 / 0.43 / 0.41, +interp 0.44 / 0.36 / 0.32.

## Reading

The along-line position is already set by the decoder's chain Viterbi through the staircase: states
every 0.25 m, the motion prior and the 1 m cells together. Finer or smoother emissions add noise
faster than they add resolution at this amount of data. The final model keeps the shipped emissions.
