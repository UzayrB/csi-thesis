# Tracking attack, stage D3: decoder and decision. Closing report

Brief: `reports/BRIEF_tracking_attack_2026-09-28.md`. Log: `reports/DEVLOG_TRACKING_2026-09-28.md`, D3.
Closed 2026-09-29 06:48 SAST. **Decision: ADOPT the relative motion gate (relgateU). Keep the display
lock and every shipped constant.**

## Method

`pipeline/constrained/pd_decode.py` re-decodes a finished cell under decode variants with no
retraining: committed, both locks and the drawn-dot resolution, configuration A. Its `ref` variant
must reproduce the cell's own rows. It did, 120/120 committed and 120/120 locks, on every rotation
cell it was run on.

The options were explored first on the pooled networks (`d1sp`), because D2 was still running. The
DECISIONS were then made on D2's confirmed networks (`d2pc_ar`), as the brief requires. Options:
- product fusion at w (and for the lock only);
- the causal null;
- the on-a-line head (a logistic regression on the frozen embeddings);
- the relative gate, in three forms;
- abstain against the veto;
- the lock method;
- the pace re-fit;
- the offset, margin, A2 weight and display spread.

## Findings

1. **The shipped null was already causal.** `g2_multihead.pool` renormalises each window so the best
   state scores 0, so the "whole-recording 75th percentile" reference is identically 0.
2. **The absolute motion gate breaks in a quiet room.** Room B's idle band energy is 2 to 80 times
   below Room A's. The gate called Room B walkers still, and the veto forbade their chains.
3. **The fix had to be designed carefully.** G0 over all training recordings mixed the rooms and
   scaled Room A down (A2: -22 committed and -25 lock passes). Referencing Room A alone still scaled
   A2 down, because A2's own idle level is 2.3 times A1's. Scaling UP only (relgateU) keeps every
   Room A rotation whole and all of Room B's gain.
4. **On the pooled networks the scorer lock helped** (+3.75). On the confirmed networks it does not
   (-0.21). Decisions made on the wrong networks would have kept it.
5. **Product fusion lifts the lock within Room A** (A2 45 -> 67.5) but costs committed answers and the
   edge lines. It is not adopted in either form.
