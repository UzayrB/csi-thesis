## 1. What was measured

**The statistic** is the app's own motion gate (`gate.band_energy`): the 0.3 to 2 Hz band power of a 2 s window,
averaged over subcarriers and links, on the window as stored. It is used in two forms:
- **RAW:** that energy.
- **SCALED, as the app scales it:** raw x max(1, G0 / g_rec).
  - **G0** = 0.754 is the final model's reference: the median, over Room A's training recordings, of each
    recording's median idle energy.
  - **g_rec** is the recording's own median over its idle windows before the first press, taken from the app's
    committed causal replays.
  - **The resulting scales** run from 1.00 to 79.83: A1 1.00 to 2.85, A2 1.00 to 1.78, A3 1.00 to 4.12,
    B1 2.26 to 41.20, B2 2.42 to 79.83.
  - **A check on g_rec:** the window tree's g_rec agrees with the app's to within
    0.37 %. The app fits each receiver's clock
    causally, so its pre-press windows sit marginally off the tree's.

**The app's threshold** is 0.431 on the scaled energy, applied unchanged. It separates walking (above) from not
walking (below); it was never an empty-room detector.

**The windows** are the 0.5 s press-aligned trees, which equal the app's live windows bit for bit
(`tests/test_live_stream.py`).
- **The trees:** fin_B2's for A1, A2, B1 and B2; fin_A3's for A3.
- **The recordings:** 30, six per session, with the calibration recordings left out.
- **No cache was written:** B1's energies had no cache and were computed in place.

**The three states,** per recording:
- **empty:** the recording's own empty-room baseline capture (60 s), cut into 2 s windows at 0.5 s and cleaned
  against its own shape, which is the shape the recording itself is cleaned against.
  - **A check:** cutting a recording with this same code reproduces the tree's energies exactly (zero difference on
    60 windows each of A1 and B2; `validate_cut.json`).
- **still:** the idle windows before the first press (the app's own rule), plus Room A's standing block (25 grid
  cells, 10 s each, head and tail discarded). Room B has no standing block (the DC2 design).
- **walking:** the labelled walk windows.

**The AUROC intervals** are a bootstrap over recordings, 1,000 draws, seed 0. The recording is the unit, since its
windows share a baseline, a day and a person.

## 3. What it shows

**Walking is detected essentially perfectly.**
- **Against the empty room:** AUROC 1.000 in every Room A session, 0.995 and 0.996 raw in B1 and B2.
- **At the app's own threshold:** 99.3 % of Room A's
  and 98.7 % of Room B's walking windows are called walking.

**A person standing still is the hard case.**
- **Room A's standing block against the empty room:** AUROC 0.793 raw
  (0.76 to 0.85 by session).
- **Against walking,** the standing block separates well: 0.978.
- **The cause:** a standing person moves a little, so the band energy sits between the empty room's and walking's,
  overlapping both tails.

**Empty against occupied** (still plus walking):
- **The AUROC:** 0.87 to 0.93 in Room A and 0.96 to 0.98 in Room B, raw.
- **Why Room B scores higher:** its "still" is pre-press idle alone (it has no standing block). That idle sits
  further above its empty room (raw median 0.098 against 0.009) than Room A's standing block does above Room A's
  (0.048 against 0.013).

**Room A's "idle before the first press" is not stillness.**
- **The energies:** raw median 0.61 against the standing block's 0.048, near walking's 1.48.
- **How well it separates:** the standing block separates from it at AUROC
  0.854.
- **So the brief's "still"** is a mix of two kinds in Room A. The tables above give both.
- **Why it matters:** these are exactly the windows the app takes g_rec from.

**The app's threshold is a walking detector, not a presence detector.**
- **As empty against occupied in Room A,** it finds only
  53.9 % of
  occupied windows, essentially the walking ones.
- **Room B by construction:**
  - The scaling maps each recording's median pre-press idle energy to G0 = 0.754, which sits above the 0.431
    threshold. So whenever a recording is scaled up, more than half its idle windows read as walking.
  - Hence Room B's still windows are called still only
    26.4 % of the time.
  - The empty baseline is scaled up with them:
    84.6 % of Room B's empty windows stay
    below 0.431.
- **Where G0 came from:** Room A's own pre-press windows, which are not still. That is why 0.754 lies above the
  walking threshold.

**An empty-against-occupied threshold** (fixed on A1+A2+B1, reported on A3 and B2):
- **Raw** (0.0688): it transfers fairly: balanced accuracy
  73.3 % on A3 and
  93.9 % on B2. It detects
  every walking window and about half the still ones.
- **Scaled:** it does not transfer to B2 (empty called empty
  23.4 %).
  B2's scales (up to 80 x) lift its empty windows over the threshold.
- **So:** the app's relative scaling suits telling walking apart across rooms, but not presence. A presence threshold
  belongs on the raw energy, or on each recording's own baseline.

**The existing per-session rule** (`gate.presence_threshold`: 1.04 x the 75th percentile of the recording's own
baseline; fixed on session 01 on 11 September; not in the app) reaches a balanced accuracy of
81.1 % in Room A and
88.2 % in Room B. Its weak case is again
standing still.

**For the thesis:**
- **Presence is detected reliably when the person moves:** walking against the empty room is AUROC 0.99 to 1.00
  everywhere.
- **Presence is detected only weakly when the person stands still:** empty against standing still is 0.76 to 0.85
  in Room A.
- **The weak case is a measured limit** of a 0.3 to 2 Hz energy statistic on three amplitude links. It is not a
  tuning failure.
- **Nothing here changes the app.**
