# V2 development round: STOPPED by its own rule. Nothing adopted; the 15:39 model stays the result

Written 30 September 2026, 00:39 SAST (Uzayr's part B).

**What was run, and what was not.** The round was pre-registered in `runs/dc2_results/rule/V2_PREREG.md`,
hashed 29 September 22:49 (SHA-256 9bb52b68...) and read-only before anything was trained. It ran on the
development rotations only. **No A3 or B2 decode was made; there is no fourth look.**
`rule/FINAL_CONFIG.md` and every file of the 15:39 model are unchanged: their hashes were checked at the
start and end of every chain.

## What was done

**1. The base, V2-B.** The 15:39 configuration, with the transit and late-entry terms in 0.5 s units
(clock 16). Item 2 (`reports/DT_MISMATCH_2026-09-30.md`) showed that this changes no decision. So V2-B
equals the 15:39 configuration on the rotations: objective 121.25 with the frozen A1+A2 pace.

**2. Ten new networks, final recipe** (a shared encoder with learned fusion, augmentation and relative
RSSI). They were trained on the GPU while Identification's lane was paused (its handover, 23:01 to
23:45), in `runs/dc2_results/v2/nets/`:
- g25 on {A1}, {A2} and {B1}: 2.5 to 3.0 min each;
- position regression on the seven session sets: 2.8 to 7.9 min each, the loss falling from about 2.0
  to 2.4 to about 1.0 to 1.2.

**3. The reader rows,** cross-fitted as `pd_clean`: 9 network-set by session pairs, 120 passes each,
in `runs/dc2_results/v2/rows/`.
- **The check:** every training row comes from a network that never saw that session, and every
  validation row from the rotation's own training sessions. It is asserted in `pd_v2.install_v2` before
  each decode.

**4. Option 3 and option 4, decoded on the three rotations** by `pipeline/constrained/pd_v2.py`, and
judged by `pd_rule` exactly as rounds 1 and 2.

**The code was smoke-tested in scratch first,** with random networks, before any real run. Option 4's
recomputed drawn paths were asserted equal to the decoder's own on every pass of every decode.

### Option 3, the readers on the final recipe: KEEP BASE

**The grid.** 12 variants (w_A2 in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, theta in {0.80, 0.90}, w_ab in {0.25, 0.5}),
with objectives from 122.29 to 123.54. The candidate is the first 123.54 in the pre-registered order.

**The rule.** The paired gain over V2-B is **+2.29 [-0.21, 5.00]**, and the edge guard holds. The
interval's lower bound is not above 0, so the base is kept.

**What it did.** It moved committed answers only: +5 passes on B1, +2 on A2 and -1 on A1. The lock is
unchanged, because start terms never reach the display lock.

### Option 4, the lock-time corner term: KEEP BASE, on the edge guard

**The grid.** w4 in {0.5, 1.0, 2.0}, with objectives 127.50, 126.25 and 126.46. The candidate is 0.5.

**The gain.** The paired gain over V2-B is **+6.25 [2.71, 9.79]**, an interval above 0. The lock
gained 20 passes (10 on B1, 3 on A2, 7 on A1), and the committed answers were unchanged.

**Why it was not adopted.** **The edge guard fails on one line: E_row5's lock recall pooled over the
rotations fell from 55.6 percent (10 of 18) to 27.8 percent (5 of 18), exactly half.** The rule counts
"half its base recall or less" as a failure. Option 4 is not adopted, and nothing was re-tuned to rescue
it.

**The corner-pair lock errors** (pooled, true -> locked), 61 before and 41 after:

|---|---|---|
| C_fwd -> E_col1 | 16 | 4 |
| D_fwd -> E_row5 | 11 | 2 |
| D_rev -> E_col5 | 8 | 5 |
| E_row5 -> D_fwd | 5 | **10** |
| E_col1 -> C_fwd | 6 | 6 |

| E_col1 -> E_row1 | 0 | 1 |

**The reading.** The corner term fixes most of the diagonal-locked-as-edge errors, the ones the 15:39
lock makes most often. But in the NW corner it over-corrects: E_row5 walks now lock as D_fwd twice as
often. That is the failure the guard exists to catch.

## The stop rule (V2_PREREG 6)

Neither option was adopted, so the round stops:
- no zero-shot V2 was built;
- no FINAL_CONFIG_V2.md was frozen;
- no A3 or B2 data were read;
- no V2 commit.

**The 15:39 model stays the result:** A3 93.33 / 70.83 (lock optimistic), B2 93.33 / 72.50, zero-shot B2
80.00 / 49.17. A3 and B2 remain decoded three times.

**What it leaves for a future round, as a note and not a choice.** The option 4 evidence says a
lock-time corner term buys lock accuracy on the rotations, but not safely for E_row5. Any change to it
would be a new pre-registered option, judged on the rotations, not a re-tune of this one.
