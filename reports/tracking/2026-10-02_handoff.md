## 1. What tracking does

From the Wi-Fi channel state of three receivers and **one key press as each walk starts**, the live app names
**which of twelve taped lines** a person is walking, and draws a dot on that line. It is told nothing else about
the walk: not its line, its end, its duration or its pace. Chance is 1 in 12, **8.33 %**.

**What it reports per walk:**

| output | when | what |
|---|---|---|
| **committed line** | at the app's own causal exit, near the end of the walk (median 1.20 s after the last tape mark on B2) | the line, decided on the whole walk |
| **lock** | at press + 2.0 s | the line the dot is locked to on screen |
| **the dot** | every frame, drawn 2.0 s behind real time | a decoded position on the locked line |

**The three modes** (`2026-09-30_live-app-model.md` section 1):
- **semi-constrained:** one start press per walk; **the headline**, and every headline figure;
- **fully constrained:** start and end press; an upper bound, never the headline;
- **unconstrained:** no press at all, the app detects starts itself (m = 4); the weakest.

**Never given in any mode:** presses 2 to 6, the walk's duration, labels, pace features, elapsed time.

## 2. The rig and the data

**The rig** (identical in both rooms; `2026-09-29_architecture.md` section 1). Metres, origin at the bottom-left of
a taped 5 m x 5 m grid.

| node | position | role |
|---|---|---|
| TX | (2.5, -0.5) | one ESP32, ESP-NOW broadcast, channel 11, HT20, about 150 packets/s |
| RX1 | (2.5, 5.5) | link 0 |
| RX2 | (-0.5, 2.5) | link 1 (its "home"; identification moves it beside the TX for its own walks) |
| RX3 | (5.5, 2.5) | link 2 |

- **What is used:** amplitude only, never phase. The receivers' gain control removes absolute level, so the shape
  across subcarriers carries the information; RSSI is a separate channel.
- **Capture:** one host process, one monotonic clock, so key presses and CSI share a clock (`pipeline/io/`).

**The twelve lines:**
- **The lines:** `A_fwd`/`A_rev` (x = 2.5), `B_fwd`/`B_rev` (y = 2.5), the diagonals `C_fwd`/`C_rev` (SW to NE)
  and `D_fwd`/`D_rev` (NW to SE), and the four edge lines `E_col1` (x = 0.5), `E_col5` (x = 4.5), `E_row1`
  (y = 0.5) and `E_row5` (y = 4.5). The edge lines run one way only.
- **The decoder's states:** every 0.25 m of arc, 288 in all.
- **Shared start corners:** SW holds `C_fwd`, `E_col1` and `E_row1`; SE holds `D_rev` and `E_col5`; NW holds
  `D_fwd` and `E_row5`. These pairs are where the lock goes wrong (section 4.2).

**The data** (`data/` is never edited):

| session | room, day | role | recordings | data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | A, day 1 | training | 6 walk recordings | `data/room_A/session_01` |
| A2 | A, day 2 | training | 6 | `data/room_A/session_02` |
| A3 | A, day 3 | **test only** | 6 (`*_TEST_walk`) | `data/room_A/session_03` |
| B1 | B, day 1 | training | 6, plus a calibration walk **excluded from training** | `data/room_B/session_B1` |
| B2 | B, day 2 | **test only** | 6, plus a calibration walk **never evaluated** | `data/room_B/session_B2` |

- **A walk recording** holds 20 passes: every A to D line twice and every E line once, in a shuffled order. So each
  session has 120 scored walks, and each edge line has 6 walks per session.
- **Truth:** the operator presses SPACE at six tape marks per pass. The first press is the start press; the labels
  use the raw press times, so the operator's 41 to 51 ms reaction bias is left in (about 5 to 7 cm).
- **Room differences:** Room A recordings have a 25-cell standing block mid-recording; Room B's are walking only.
  Each recording has an empty-room baseline capture.
- **People:** PA5, PA1, PA2, PA3, PA4, PA6. New outputs use names. **Tracking's old reports use
  P-codes from `runs/dc2_results/teaching/anon_map.json`, which differ from identification's codes** (tracking's P5
  is PA2). Read the map; never carry a code across the two branches.

## 3. The final model, step by step

One model, in `pipeline/live/final_model.py` (`FinalTrackingSession`, `DISPLAY_CANDIDATE = "K1g"`). The configuration
is frozen in `runs/dc2_results/rule/FINAL_CONFIG.md` (hashed). The offline code (`pipeline/constrained/pd_decode`,
`pd_render`, `drawn_errors`) is a cross-check only. Every step below is in `2026-09-29_architecture.md`, read from
the code.

1. **Parse** each receiver's rows: 128 complex CSI values and RSSI per packet.
2. **Clean, per receiver:**
   - drop 21 dead bins, leaving 107 subcarriers;
   - normalise each half-packet's shape by its own mean;
   - Hampel-despike;
   - resample to a shared 150 Hz grid;
   - subtract the empty-room baseline shape.
3. **Window:** 2 s (300 samples x 107 x 3 receivers) every 0.5 s, the lattice re-phased so a window starts on
   each start press.
4. **Features, per link:**
   - **channel 0:** a 2 Hz zero-phase low-pass of the shape;
   - **channel 1:** **relative RSSI**, minus the recording's idle mean before its first press;
   - **per window:** one scalar, the 0.3 to 2 Hz band energy, for the motion gate.
5. **Five networks.** Each is one `LinkCNN` encoder (80,000 parameters) shared across the three links, with a fusion
   head over 25 one-metre cells (one head over 100 half-metre cells).
   - **Training:** on A1 + A2 + B1 walking windows, 24 epochs, with augmentation (RSSI offset, shape scale, time
     shift, noise).
   - **Strides:** one head at 0.5 s, three seeds at 1 s, and the 100-cell head.
6. **The pool:** each head's log posterior minus the training marginal, mapped onto the 288 line states, averaged
   with equal weight, and the best state set to 0 each window.
7. **The relative motion gate (relgateU):**
   - band energy x max(1, G0 / g_rec), with G0 = 0.7542 (A1 + A2) and g_rec the recording's own idle median before
     its first press;
   - below 0.431 the window is "still", and every line state is forbidden.
8. **The start-of-walk readers,** at each start press:
   - **the A2 region reader** (weight 0.25);
   - **the abstain reader**, in place of the old veto, used only when at least 0.90 sure (weight 0.25).
   - Their rows are cross-fitted (the 29 September leak fix).
   - **They are the old per-link networks with absolute RSSI**, never retrained on the final recipe.
   - **They feed the committed line only, never the lock.**
9. **The decoder:** a 418-state HMM (288 line states, STILL and TRANSIT states), told only when a walk starts.
   - **Entry:** soft entry in the first 1 m of every line.
   - **Pace prior:** N(1.30341, 0.11255) m/s, from the A1 + A2 tape anchors.
   - **The causal exit:** the walk ends at the first window whose best state is off every line.
10. **The committed line:** at the exit, per-line Viterbi totals plus the chain prior and the start terms; the
    best decision group (vert, horz, diagC, diagD, edge) first, then its best member.
11. **The lock:** at press + 2.0 s, the line of a fixed-lag display decode (spread x4.5, step cap 0.8 m per window).
    It names the position about 1.3 m into the walk.
12. **The dot, display K1g** (since 1 October 00:02): look-ahead of 1 window, with the anchor gliding off the line's
    entry. The dot is a decoded position on the locked line, drawn 2.0 s behind real time.
    - **What K1g changes:** the drawn dot only. No lock and no committed line differs from K0's.

**Live specifics** (`final_model.py`, `2026-09-29_architecture.md` sections 14 and 17.4):
- **At the first press,** the recording's statistics (the relative-RSSI means, g_rec, AdaBN) are fixed from its idle
  windows before the press. At least 10 are needed, or the session refuses; AdaBN needs at least 20. **So the
  walker stands still for 15 s or more before the first walk.**
- **The heads never run before the first press.**
- **The identity tag** (from identification) reaches only the screen; no tracking module reads it.

**The zero-shot model (`zsf_B2`):** the same recipe trained on **A1 + A2 only**. Its readers, marginal, chain prior,
G0 and pace also come from A1 + A2 only.

## 4. The figures

**How every figure is made:**
- **Source:** the app's own replays (`runs/live/replay/<model>/<mode>/`), on the as-it-arrives (causal) clock,
  scored by `pipeline/live/app_score.py`.
- **Walks:** 120 per test session; calibration recordings excluded.
- **Intervals:** 95 % percentile bootstrap over walks, 1,000 draws, seed 0, unless marked otherwise.
- **Partition:** the final model is trained on A1 + A2 + B1 and tested on A3 or B2. The zero-shot model is trained
  on A1 + A2 and tested on B2.
- **Calibration:** no labelled same-day calibration. Each recording's statistics come from its own idle windows
  before its first press (causal, unlabelled), and the calibration walks are never used.
- **The A3 label:** A3's lock and dot error are **OPTIMISTIC**, because display settings (the cap, the spread, the
  soft entry) were chosen on data that included A3 (`2026-09-30_display-provenance.md`). **B2 is the headline.**
- **Equality with the offline decode:** the app's answers equal the corrected offline decode on 360 of 360 walks
  (G1 and G2 exact), so the offline paired figures carry over.

## 5. Say plainly in the write-up

1. **One model, the live app.** Every figure is the app's own replay on the causal clock, and every answer equals
   the corrected offline decode (360 of 360).
2. **The headline is B2:** 93.33 % committed and 72.50 % lock, against 8.33 % chance. That is from a model trained
   on Room A's first two days and Room B's first day, tested on Room B's second day.
3. **The task is constrained.** Twelve known taped lines and a start press per walk. Without the press the app
   finds about two fifths of the walks (36.67 % correct-and-detected on B2), and a free-form 2D regressor did not
   beat a centre guess meaningfully.
4. **The lock is the weak output, the committed line the strong one.** The lock is read 2 s after the press, and
   corner-sharing lines are still together there.
5. **A3's lock and dot are optimistic.** Display settings were chosen on data that included A3.
6. **Test looks:**
   - **Before the app:** A3 and B2 were decoded three times: the midpoint, FINAL (which carried the reader leak)
     and the corrected test.
   - **Since:** the app's replays evaluate one frozen model, with nothing chosen on them.
   - **Development dropped pre-registration on 28 September.** Design choices were made on the development
     rotations (A1 + A2 to B1, A1 + B1 to A2, A2 + B1 to A1), so B1 is both training data and selection data.
7. **Cross-room:** without any Room B data the model scores 80.00 / 49.17 on B2. One day of Room B adds +13.33
   committed and +23.33 lock.
8. **K1g is Uzayr's decision on development data, taken after the pre-registered rule kept K0** (recorded in
   `runs/dc2_results/display/DOT_DECISION.md`). It moves the dot only.
9. **The rig on the boards has not run the final model with walkers.** The demonstration is B2 streamed as if
   live, through the boards' ingestion path. The boards path is kept working.
10. **Known simplifications:**
    - the start readers are old per-link networks with absolute RSSI;
    - the labels keep the operator's reaction bias;
    - the decoder's transit and late-entry quantities are in 1 s units inside a 0.5 s decoder. This was
      disclosed and is inert: 0 of 360 decisions (`2026-09-30_time-step-disclosure.md`).
11. **The gate leans on each recording's quiet start.** Room B's scales run from x2.4 to x79.7, so the room must be
    empty and still before the first press.

## 13. Pitfalls learned

1. **Use the project interpreter.** On 2 October 09:42 a fresh shell's `python3` was `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3`
   (3.14, no numpy, no pytest), and the pre-flight failed at import. `/opt/anaconda3/bin/python3` works.
   `stream_chain.sh` calls bare `python3`.
2. **An untagged output folder gets overwritten.**
   - On 1 October a default-display replay wrote K1g output into K0's folder.
   - On 30 September the spread sweep wrote over three committed `verify/clean` records. The working tree held the
     overwrite until 1 October, when it was moved aside (`e6d62dc`).
   - Tag every run's folder.
3. **Caches can write into hashed trees.** `recordings.gate_energy()` saves a cache into a cell's artefact folder
   when none exists. Compute without saving.
4. **The causal clock moves windows by milliseconds.** A streamed run flips a few borderline locks. Take the app's
   numbers from its replays.
5. **zsh does not word-split an unquoted variable,** so `for f in $files` runs once or not at all. Use
   `while read`.
6. **The scratchpad under `/private/tmp` is wiped on a reboot.** Rebuild any check list instead of trusting it.
7. **`$?` read after `$(date)`** reports the date's exit code. Capture the code first.
8. **A brief's word can disagree with the data.** Room A's "idle before the first press" is not still: its band
   energy is near walking's. Report both.
9. **Leakage enters through aliased frozen heads.** Audit decodes by tracing the files they open (the reader leak,
   29 September).
10. **Do not announce work that has not been launched.**
11. **Old paths are everywhere in old reports.** Translate them through the two moves maps; never edit the reports.
