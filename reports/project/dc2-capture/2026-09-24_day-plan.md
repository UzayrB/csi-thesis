# DC2 day plan: one page per day, in capture order

**26 and 27 September, Room B.** The design is `PREREG_DC2_ADDENDUM_2026-09-24.md` with its corrections
`..._24b.md` and `..._2026-09-25.md` (identification first, then tracking), which govern
where the pinned sheets (`DC2_FIELD_CHECKLIST.md`, `DC2_ID_PROTOCOL_2026-09-23d.md`) disagree. `Layout.png` is the
rig: the seat, and RX2 at home and at its identification spot. Every
command runs from the repo root. `NODES="RX1=<port>,RX2=<port>,RX3=<port>"`, each port matched to its
MAC with `make identify PORT=<port>`: RX1 `68:09:47:4c:ef:b4`, RX2 `20:50:0d:1a:28:74`, RX3
`20:50:0d:30:5f:80`. No RX4. **Day 1 is session `B1`, day 2 is `B2`. Never name a Room B session 01,
02 or 03.** If anything cannot be done as written, note what and why on the sheet at the time.

**Every capture ends with `RESULT: PASS` or `RESULT: FAIL`** (the stream gates: 146 packets a second or
more, under 5 % lost). A **baseline** that fails is simply taken again; `$BASE` then picks the new one.
A **walk** that fails is noted on the sheet and kept, unless a link stopped outright. A link that
stops (0 packets, or a port that will not open): unplug that board and plug it back in, run
`scripts/live_rig_run.sh --dry-run` again (port names can change), then redo the capture. On 2026-09-24 the
TX once jumped its packet count by about 490 in one minute (all three boards saw it at once) and was
clean the next minute: it happens, and the gate is what shows it.

## Before the first participant, each day (about 45 min)

1. **Geometry, Room A's:** TX (2.5, -0.5); RX1 (2.5, 5.5); RX2 home (-0.5, 2.5) facing east; RX3
   (5.5, 2.5); all at 1.1 m. Tape the 5 x 5 m grid and RX2's outline at home and at the spot.
   Operator seat (-1.0, 0.5). Photograph everything.
2. **The laptop never sleeps**: on its charger, and `caffeinate -dimsu &` in the terminal first. On
   2026-09-24 a sleep in the middle of the hardware check wedged RX2's USB adapter: its port stayed
   listed but refused every setting, and only unplugging RX2 and plugging it back in cleared it.
   **Boards:** `scripts/live_rig_run.sh --dry-run` shows RX1 to RX3 by MAC; `make run DURATION=20 NODES="$NODES"`
   shows every link near the transmit rate.
3. **Reaction time:** `python3 -m pipeline.io.rt_calibration`.
4. **Baseline, 60 s**: operator seated, nobody in the grid, still. Every tracking recording needs one;
   the DC2 cut stops without it. Then take the newest baseline folder:

       make baseline BASELINE_S=60 OUT=data/room_B/session_B1 NODES="$NODES"
       BASE=$(ls -td data/room_B/session_B1/2026*/ | head -1); echo $BASE

5. **Calibration walks**, never scored, walked by the day's first participant (day 1 PA5, day 2
   PA6) under `<name>_C` (codes are 8 characters at most). **Never `--participant CAL`**: the DC2 pipeline keeps only recordings
   named for one of the six, so a `CAL` recording is dropped without a word and configuration B quietly
   equals A. Found by the hardware check on 2026-09-24.

       python3 -m pipeline.io.ground_truth --participant PA5_C --room B --session B1 --mode walk --baseline "$BASE" \
         --nodes "$NODES" --rx-positions "RX1=2.5,5.5 RX2=-0.5,2.5 RX3=5.5,2.5" --tx-position 2.5,-0.5 \
         --chair=-1.0,0.5 --height-cm <cm> --weight-kg <kg> --clothing "<text>" --footwear "<text>"

   Walk 8 passes, then press `q` straight after the eighth pass's sixth press. Then:

       python3 -m pipeline.constrained.dc2b_manifest --session B1 --calibration participant_PA5_C_walk

   (Day 2: `session_B2`, `--session B2` and `PA6_C` throughout.) `--chair=` takes the equals sign: the value starts with a minus.

## Each participant (about 24 min): identification, then tracking

Order: day 1 P1 to P6, day 2 P6 to P1 (`PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.capture_idboth --plan-day --day <d>`).
RX2 starts every block at home, facing east. Identification comes first (addendum 2026-09-25, the
order sheet 23d pre-registered); the home empty room then sits straight before that person's tracking.

**1. RX2 to the identification spot**, (2.0, -0.5), facing north (confirmed in the room and recorded on
24 Sept; `Layout.png` shows both places). Align, photograph, note the time.

**2. Empty room at the spot, 30 s**, everyone outside the grid and still. `--move-time` = step 1's time:

    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.capture_idboth --empty --position idspot --participant <name> \
      --day <d> --move-time HH:MM --nodes "$NODES"

**3. Identification walks.** One command per block; natural first, then the printed pace order
(odd P codes slow first). Day 1: natural 10, slow 5, fast 5 cycles. Day 2: 5, 5, 5.

    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.capture_idboth --participant <name> --day <d> --block natural \
      --height-cm <cm> --weight-kg <kg> --shoes "<text>" --clothing "<text>" --nothing-carried --nodes "$NODES"

A cycle: stand still 5 s at 0 m facing north; walk away, space at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 m; stop just before
RX1, turn, stand still 5 s; walk back, space at 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 m; stop at 0 m without touching the TX
or RX2; turn to face north. Every pass lasts 1.5 to 12 s: a slow walker keeps moving. A block ending
short: same command plus `--amend-count-reason "<why>"`.

**4. RX2 home**, facing east. Align, photograph, note the time.

**5. Empty room at home, 30 s**, everyone outside the grid and still. `--move-time` = step 4's time:

    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.capture_idboth --empty --position home --participant <name> \
      --day <d> --move-time HH:MM --nodes "$NODES"

**6. Baseline, 60 s**, straight after, still nobody in the grid, operator seated (Room A took one before
every participant):

    make baseline BASELINE_S=60 OUT=data/room_B/session_B1 NODES="$NODES"
    BASE=$(ls -td data/room_B/session_B1/2026*/ | head -1); echo $BASE

**7. Tracking, RX2 home, about 8 min.** 20 passes in random order across the 12 lines, walking only:

    python3 -m pipeline.io.ground_truth --participant <name> --room B --session B1 --mode walk --baseline "$BASE" \
      --nodes "$NODES" --rx-positions "RX1=2.5,5.5 RX2=-0.5,2.5 RX3=5.5,2.5" --tx-position 2.5,-0.5 \
      --chair "seat (-1.0, 0.5), laptop on desk" --height-cm <cm> --weight-kg <kg> --clothing "<text>" --footwear "<text>"

Space at each of the six marks; `x` redoes a pass; `q` stops. A pass abandoned at capture time only:
`python3 -m pipeline.constrained.dc2b_manifest --session B1 --aborted participant_<name>_walk:<pass id>`.

**8. Check, while they are still here:** `python3 -m tools.dc2_day_check --day <d>`. It reads only.
"NOTHING WRONG" means every file DC2 needs is there; anything else says what to re-record.

## End of each day

`python3 -m tools.dc2_day_check --day <d> --final` must say NOTHING WRONG: all six, both parts.
`python3 -m pipeline.constrained.dc2b_manifest --show`: only passes abandoned at capture time are aborted. Note anything unusual. Back up `data/room_B`.

## After DC2, before any figure is quoted

    python3 -m tools.dc2_day_check --day 1 --final && python3 -m tools.dc2_day_check --day 2 --final
    python3 -m pipeline.constrained.dc2b_pipeline --room room_B        # tracking, the DC2 outcomes
    python3 -m tools.dc2_verify_run --room room_B                      # must say VERIFIED before any figure is read
    python3 -m pipeline.constrained.dc2b_scorerlock --room room_B      # exploratory display comparison
    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.idboth_view              # identification's pre-registered view
    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.id29_dc2_score --data-root runs/identify/dc2_view/room_B
    PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m pipeline.identify.idgp_dc2_score --data-root runs/identify/dc2_view/room_B

**Never `dc2_pipeline` or `dc2_scorerlock` on DC2 data**: they fail on the per-recording manifest. Never
the identification scorers on `data/room_B` directly: they refuse it. Only the commands above score.
