# DC2 capture protocol, 26 and 27 September, second room

Written to be printed and followed. Every item cites the measurement that argues for it.

## Why a second room changes the project

Cross-environment generalisation is the stated core contribution and it is currently UNTESTED rather than unreachable. A second room is the only way to test it, and it also gives the identification branch the thing it most lacks, which is a second environment in which a person's gait can be shown to survive.

## Per participant, per session

| item | amount | why |
|---|---|---|
| walking passes | **20 to 30 per person**, not 20 | the per-person calibration arm scored 8 passes each, where one found pass is 12.5 percent and the floor sits below the resolution of the measurement. That arm could not be MEASURED, which is different from failing |
| edge lines | **deliberately over-sampled** | at 1.67 passes per line per participant a random one-in-five hold-out leaves some lines with two or three passes in the entire within-day evaluation |
| **deliberate speed variation** | slow, normal and fast passes per person | THIS IS THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT ITEM FOR IDENTIFICATION. Speed separability on the current cohort is 0.51, so pace and identity are not separable by accident here; deliberately varying pace within a person is the only way to prove that any identification is gait rather than pace |
| standing | a short block of 3 to 4 minutes over a handful of cells | the 25-cell grid costs about eight minutes per person and stationary position regression already works; keep the empty-room baseline and the motion gate's presence examples |
| **labelled off-path walking** | several minutes per person, weaving, curving, stopping and starting | this is the negative class the transit false fires show is missing: the decoder fires 19 to 25 times per recording while the person walks BETWEEN lines because nothing ever taught it what that looks like |

### CALIBRATION AT THE START OF EACH DC2 DAY. Added 2026-09-22, and it is not optional

**Why this exists.** Two quantities in the decode path are absolute numbers fitted to Room A, and
neither raises when it is wrong. `reports/ROOM_TRANSFER_AUDIT_2026-09-22.md` found them; `t8_calib`
measured how much each matters, over nine pairings:

| quantity | shipped | values within one pass of shipped | tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| motion gate threshold | 0.431 band energy | **0.20, 0.30, 0.431, 0.60** | **about 3x, forgiving** |
| null level | −7.50 nats | **−9.0, −7.5** | **about 1.5 nats, tight** |

Outside those: a gate at 1.10 costs 4.2 points and **a null at −10.5 costs 16.7 points**. The null
is the dangerous one and the audit under-weighted it.

**WHAT TO RECORD, at the start of each day, before any evaluated pass.**

| block | duration | why |
|---|---|---|
| **empty room, nobody inside** | 60 s | the noise floor for the gate, and the reference the whole day scales against |
| **standing still, one person, room centre** | 60 s | the STILL end of the gate's bracket |
| **calibration walks, 2 passes on each of 4 named lines** | about 3 min | the WALKING end of the bracket, AND the only thing that exercises what the null discriminates |
| a note of the time each block started | | so the causality check can prove they precede every pass |

**The calibration walks are the part that cannot be skipped**, and Room A is the proof. Room A has
an empty-room capture but no calibration walks, so `t8_calib` had to improvise an anchor from the
windows before each recording's first press. That period is the participant arriving and the
operator setting up, not a controlled reference: its median band energy varies **2.3x across three
days in the same room**, and the relative forms built on it did NOT reproduce the shipped figure
within one pass per pairing. They were unbiased, paired deltas of +0.00 to +1.67 with intervals
straddling zero, but scattered by up to 3.33 points, because on the days with a busy setup period
the derived threshold landed outside the flat region.

**The failure is the anchor, not the formula.** That is why the fix is a protocol change rather
than a better equation.

**HOW THE CONSTANTS ARE THEN SET**, using only the calibration blocks:

```
still  = median band energy of the standing block
walk   = median band energy of the calibration walks
theta(day) = still + f * (walk - still)          f fitted on Room A, dimensionless
null(day)  = -(7.50 / spread_ref) * spread(day)  spread = median over the calibration walks'
                                                 windows of (emission row max - row median)
```

Both are **dimensionless multiples of that day's own measurements**, which is what transfers where
an absolute level does not. The code is `pipeline/constrained/t8_calib.py`.

**THEY ARE NOT ENABLED BY DEFAULT.** They did not clear the one-pass bar on Room A's improvised
anchor, so they stay off until a real calibration block exists. **On DC2 day one, before any figure
is quoted: re-run the verification with the day's own calibration block, and enable the relative
forms only if they reproduce within the same one-pass bar.** If they do not, fall back to the
absolute constants and report every DC2 number with the calibration check printed beside it.

**THE THREE NUMBERS TO PRINT BESIDE ANY DC2 FIGURE**, which turn both silent failures into loud
ones and cost nothing:

1. the pooled emission scale, median over windows of (row max minus row median);
2. the realised null reference, which under the five-head pool is 0.000 in Room A and must be
   checked rather than assumed in Room B;
3. the band-energy distribution, p10, p50 and p90, for the empty room, the standing block and the
   calibration walks.

If Room B's numbers sit far outside Room A's, the decoder has not failed; it has been handed
constants that do not describe the room it is in.

### The ship rule that DC2 analyses run under. Amended 2026-09-22

An arm ships only if **(a)** its paired improvement interval on the held-out folds is entirely
above zero, **(b)** the median paired delta is at least **two passes per pairing**, and **(c)** the
effect shows a **monotone response to the term's own weight**.

Recorded as a rule change made after seeing that condition (a) alone can fire at the resolution
floor: `t5_shipc` returned an interval of [0.24, 1.07] excluding zero on an effect that was five
passes in 840, with no weight gradient. Canonical copy in `reports/SEMIBOUNDED_STATE.md` section 10.

**At 120 passes a pairing, two passes is 1.67 points.** If DC2 changes the passes per pairing, (b)
is TWO PASSES and the points figure follows from the new count; do not carry 1.67 across.

### The approach walk. Added 2026-09-21 after the pre-press region work

**The finding.** Room A holds 1440 pre-press windows, exactly four per pass, covering a 3.5 s span
that ends a median 0.282 s before the press. Every one is labelled `walk_edge` and **not one carries a position label**, so no
head on record has ever seen a participant outside the grid
(`pipeline/constrained/dataset.py:121` selects `mode == "walk"` and a finite `xy`).

That matters because the start region would be worth a great deal. `t0_oracle` measured a perfect
start-region reader worth **+12.5 points** of line accuracy over the seven held-out pairings, with
an interval of [9.5, 26.2], and the gain is spread across all eight regions rather than carried by
one. But `t1_region` then measured the region from those same windows at **25.0 percent against a
20.0 percent best-constant baseline**, which is nowhere near the **74.2 percent** that the same
oracle sweep says a region term must reach before it stops costing line accuracy.

**So the retrofit failed, and the protocol is the fix.** The approach happens outside the taped
grid, where there is no ground truth and, on the A and B lines, only 0.20 to 0.25 m of clearance
before a node. Room A cannot answer this question because Room A never labelled it.

**What DC2 should capture.**

- **Record the approach.** Do not begin the capture at the press. The two to four seconds of walk-in
  are already in the radio; what is missing is the label.
- **Label its start region**, which is free: it is the known start region of the pass about to be
  walked, so it needs no new measurement, only that the windows are not discarded.
- **Give the approach a position track where the video allows it**, so the approach zone becomes
  trainable as position rather than only as a region class.
- **Mark the approach zone geometry per line**, including how much clear run-up each line has before
  a node blocks it. In Room A that ranged from 0.20 m to 1.5 m and the short ones are the lines the
  decoder confuses most.
- **Vary where the approach comes from.** In Room A the previous pass's end predicted the next
  start on only 11.7 percent of passes, which is why that confound stayed small. Keep it small
  deliberately rather than by luck, so an approach-trained head cannot learn the pass order.

Brief and full numbers: `reports/PREREG_prepress_region_2026-09-21.md`,
`runs/constrained/fh/t0_oracle/results.json`, `runs/constrained/fh/t1_region/results.json`.

## What the identification result asks for specifically

Three-person identification reached 50.5 percent against 33.3 chance, and six-person is marginal. If identification is to be a thesis contribution rather than a negative, DC2 should either commit to a small cohort and measure it properly, or accept many more passes per person so that a six-way result has the sample size to be measurable. The current 20 passes per person per session is the binding constraint on every per-person arm in this report.

The gait extraction also needs 115 of 360 passes' worth of asymmetry explained. Video would settle whether the stride-rate dominance is a real gait asymmetry or an artefact of the envelope.
