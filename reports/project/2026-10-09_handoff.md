## 7. Pitfalls learned on 8 and 9 October

- **zsh BG_NICE:** `cmd &` runs at nice +5, and a child's `nice -n 5` stacks on top. Niced work lands on the efficiency
  cores.
- **One caffeinate per stage let the Mac sleep** when a stage ended (about 3 h lost overnight). On battery, `-s` holds
  nothing.
- **Cells registered in memory** must be registered in every subprocess. `inproc.py` does this for `pd_clean` and
  `pd_cells` commands. `pd_cells.DEV3` is `("01", "02", "B1")`, so sort before comparing lists of cells. A check that
  did not sort stopped the grid once.
- **The d1sp twin cells were removed in the 6 Oct cleanup.** A cell built on them takes its readers from
  `d4c_fuse_*`'s byte copies (alias `V0`).
- **The unconstrained replay folders** need `app_score.score_unconstrained(..., "disp-K1g_causal")`, not the semi
  scorer.
- **This shell's zsh does not take the `(N)` glob qualifier,** and the Bash tool blocks `sleep N; ...` chains. Use a
  Monitor with an until-loop, or a background command.
- **Monitor patterns:** `pgrep -f` matches its own command line, so watch a PID instead.
