## Room A's 1 s windows moved out of `data/` (23:25 SAST)

`data/windows/` (3.2 GB, 38 files: `room_A/session_01` to `_03`) is a derived 1 s-stride window cache, cut 10 to
14 September by `pipeline.io.windows`, not captured data, so Uzayr asked for it to leave `data/`. It is now at
**`runs/constrained/windows_s1.0_roomA/`** (beside Room B's `windows_s1.0_dc2/`; git-ignored by
`runs/constrained/windows*/`), and **`data/windows` is a symlink to it**.

- **Why a symlink and not new paths in the code:** the windows are a training input of the final tracker's 1 s
  heads (`g1s1`, `g1s2`, `g100`). The default path `data/windows` is written into `pipeline/constrained/recordings.py`

  and the EXACT reproduction. It is also the default of `pipeline/io/windows.py` (never edited),
  `tools/calibrate_gate.py` and `idmax_common.py`, and `tests/test_stride_isolation.py` asserts it.
