## 6. Pitfalls learned in this chat

- **zsh does not split `$P`** for `kill`: use `${=P}` or `kill $(pgrep ...)`. A failed stop once left two drivers running.
- **`pgrep -f` matches watcher scripts** whose command line contains the pattern; count with `ps -A -o command=` and a
  `[/]`-prefixed grep, and ignore one-off duplicates from fork-then-exec.
- **`pd_repro_trace`'s decode step exits 1 whenever answers differ from the shipped record**, which is normal for any
  other model; check its outputs, not the exit code.
- **A decode scratch tree holds reader networks beside the cell's heads:** swap only files with the cell's `heads_tag`.
- **Background tool commands stop after two hours;** run long jobs with `nohup` and watch their log.
- **Niced CPU jobs land on efficiency cores** and run about four times slower; prioritise the critical path.
- **The Bin is not readable** from the shell, and its 1 Oct contents are now deleted.
